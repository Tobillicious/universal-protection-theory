## How to use the map

1. Pick a question or theorem, and record its exact file, statement, assumptions, and revision.
2. State what is mathematically checked, what is an ethical commitment, and what is a physical hypothesis.
3. Look for counterexamples that satisfy the actual hypotheses; document them without dismissing stronger statements.
4. Add a reproducible test or a specific missing bridge, not a vague assertion.
5. Preserve the original six X3 and historical summit sources. Propose additive changes for review.

This map should grow through contributions from diverse perspectives, including disagreements.
