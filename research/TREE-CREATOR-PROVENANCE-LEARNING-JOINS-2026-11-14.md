## 3. Proposed joins (none claimed complete)

### J1 — Complete source preservation → nonmerging genealogy

Specify a candidate successor state that retains **enough parent identity, branch choice, and ordered event history** to recover distinguishable parent trajectories. Do not assume that preservation of `WholeHasIntrinsicKoru` entails injectivity of a successor selector. Reuse the existing adversarial merge and branch-bit repairs; audit whether they generalize beyond the two witnessed parents. **Countertest:** two distinct parents map to one child after erasing the provenance field.

### J2 — Genealogy → epistemically honest feedback

Connect `KnowledgeSeed` histories to `JokerExperiment.completedHistory` with an explicit history-fidelity condition, so `revise` cannot silently rewrite or omit the evidence used to evaluate a candidate theory. Distinguish (i) predicted history, (ii) measured/represented history, (iii) revised hypothesis, and (iv) proposed successor. **Countertest:** one passing experiment while a second history falsifies the same theory; a revised theory that discards the failed record.

### J3 — Feedback → original-six protection of actual particulars

A candidate next-world choice must be assessed against the *unchanged* six original duties at the **same event and owed particular**. Source tags, records and mutual-hand edges are not genuine consent. Specify independent semantic reflection and physical coverage interfaces; refuse a theorem that assumes `OriginalX3` inside the definition of “lawful genesis.” **Countertest:** mutually linked source nodes with false authentic authorization; a perfectly archived history whose subject loses future freedom.

### J4 — Hume–Archimedes → Creator's responsibility

Philosophical proposal: a creator's power to model or enact a world does not itself confer authorization to impose its risks. A creator remains corrigible to evidence from created particulars. To formalize this, identify the independently justified ethical principle and its correspondence to the actual-world source **without inserting the desired conclusion as an axiom**. Treat the creator/next-U interpretation as a hypothesis, not established cosmology or theology. **Countertest:** a mathematically elegant successor that violates one original X3 field.

### J5 — First Koru / Complete Koru / Time Koru → thermodynamic route

Coordinate with [PR #437](https://github.com/Tobillicious/universal-protection-theory/pull/437). Keep spacetime and matterspacetime semantics separate; preserve ordered operators and histories; distinguish global microscopic information retention from local entropy production. A thermodynamic bridge requires actual dynamics and independently measured correspondence, not isometry alone. No entropy reversal, heat-death genesis, or physical wormhole is established here.

## 4. Reviewer challenge: the strongest noncircular route

For each proposed join, request: (1) exact Lean theorem name and pinned commit; (2) all hypotheses, with target-equivalent premises flagged; (3) a nonempty positive witness; (4) a hostile countermodel; (5) kernel build, axiom audit and complete CI receipt; (6) an explicit account of physical/semantic evidence still missing.

**Adversarial questions for Claude or any independent reviewer:**

1. Does J1 preserve every distinguishable source history or only a chosen witness pair?
2. Does J2 prove observation fidelity, or merely copy a supplied history value?
3. Does J3 independently derive authentic authorization and future freedom, or rename them as source relations?
4. Does J4 cross Hume's is/ought gap using an independently justified premise, or presuppose guardianship?
5. Does J5 justify the physics of a next universe, or only construct an internally consistent model?
6. Can an actual owed particular lose one of the original six duties while all proposed source-preservation statements remain true?

A valid counterexample must be retained as evidence, not dismissed merely because it occurs within U. An actual objection is *within* the proposed whole, but its truth is decided by its content.
