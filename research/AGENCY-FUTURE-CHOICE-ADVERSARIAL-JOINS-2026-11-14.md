## A. Root distinction: preservation is not self-determination

The original six X3 duties are **historical continuity, authentic authorization, freedom, least-necessary restraint, feasible maximality, and provenance**, jointly for every owed particular at every actual event. See [OriginalX3Full.lean](../evidence/physical-bridges/PhysicalBridges/OriginalX3Full.lean) and [HILLARY-ROUTE-A-B-SUMMIT.md](../HILLARY-ROUTE-A-B-SUMMIT.md).

The author's invocation of *tino rangatiratanga* motivates a philosophical and relational review of self-determination, including collective dimensions. It must **not** be silently identified with a Boolean authorization field, an isolated individual's choice count, or an exhaustive formal definition of the Māori concept. Any culturally grounded equivalence would need its own appropriate sources and engagement.

**Candidate necessary distinction:** a process can faithfully preserve a particular's past record while removing all meaningful future options. Thus `historyRecordPreserved` alone must not discharge `freedom`, `authorization`, or `maximality`. This is a proposed countermodel design, not an already checked theorem.

## C. Proposed review interfaces, not adopted axioms

For each independently owed particular `p` and actual event `e`, a future-choice claim requires at least:

1. **Identity/continuity:** show that the *same* particular is being protected, rather than a replacement record.
2. **Authentic authorization:** independent subject-meaning correspondence; neither source mutuality nor a stored flag suffices.
3. **Meaningful options:** define and validate a feasible protective family in the actual context, not merely a nonempty abstract set.
4. **Proportionate restraint:** identify why each restriction is necessary and how it can be reviewed or lifted.
5. **Feasible maximality:** compare within the *actual* feasible protective family, with the original order and obligations unchanged.
6. **Provenance:** retain decisions, relevant alternatives, source histories and evidence of failure without silently merging them.

This section restates the **existing six** as review questions. It is not a replacement six-X3 definition.

## D. Adversarial tests to construct

**T1 — Archive without agency.** Construct a source/history-preserving transition whose subject has no meaningful future choice. Confirm that any proposed implication `recordPreserved → originalSix` is refuted or requires independent premises.

**T2 — Mutual hand without consent.** Reuse the existing `reciprocal_physical_hand_alone_does_not_force_consent` control. A new join must not infer authorization from a two-way operator relation.

**T3 — Choice without feasible protection.** A nominal menu of options can coexist with a lack of feasible, safe or authorized alternatives. The proof must use the actual feasible family rather than counting branches.

**T4 — Holding without ownership.** In the hostile-agent reasons-or-faithful-holding case, show how proportionate intervention protects others while respecting the held particular's remaining rights and opportunities for reassessment. Holding must not become indefinite control merely by changing a label.

**T5 — History collision.** Two distinct parent states lead to the same child when branch information is forgotten. Demand an explicit provenance-fidelity interface, and test more than the already constructed witness pair.

**T6 — Collective and relational self-determination.** Identify where individual-only models fail to represent group decisions, relationships and shared responsibilities. This is a research question, not a theorem about tikanga or an assertion that one universal Boolean captures tino rangatiratanga.

**T7 — Counterexample retention.** If a test fails, retain the failing trace and its source identity in the knowledge seed. A learning system that deletes its refutations is not epistemically trustworthy.

## E. Noncircular proof obligation

A future candidate theorem must have the schematic shape:

`independentlyVerifiedPhysicalCoverage ∧ independentlyVerifiedAuthenticSubjectSemantics ∧ separatelyProvedSourceAndOptionInvariants → originalSixForEveryActualEventAndOwedParticular`

This is **only a target schema**. The antecedents must not be defined as synonyms for `originalSix`; equivalence lemmas alone cannot supply independent evidence. The exact event, subject and six obligations must remain unchanged.
