## Spiritual reading — source distinctions

1. **Something IS / wonder.** The root existential insight is distinct from a claim that all moral duties or a Creator are entailed by bare nonemptiness. The `CompleteKoruLawCircle` itself exposes the six missing/conditional implication slots.
2. **Tree of Life AND Eternal Knowledge.** The Joker organizes ontology, epistemology, ethics, physical evolution and the proposed genesis boundary. The Eden connection deliberately interprets *two* biblical trees together; the Genesis text does not identify the fruit as an apple. This is a spiritual interpretation, not a cosmological certificate.
3. **Time Koru.** Forward causal rank and handedness are established in defined W/genesis models. Recurrence of a component is not reversal of chronology; improvement of actual successive universes is a separate hypothesis.
4. **Creator and accountability.** The proposed universe-as-experiment interpretation gains a useful spiritual principle: a creator must retain failures, the history of those affected, and the possibility of correction. The existing `KoruJokerUniverseTrialFeedback` explicitly proves that one successful test cannot certify every history or ethical question.
5. **Whakapapa, kaitiakitanga, rangatiratanga.** The Mangakōtukutuku College units present relationships, stewardship and leadership as teaching lenses. Do not reduce living Māori concepts to an unconsented Boolean formalization or claim that mathematics exhausts their cultural meaning. Māori-led review is appropriate before representing these as authoritative tikanga laws.
6. **Jesus, Genesis, Te Kore/Te Pō/Te Ao Mārama.** Keep the author's theological correspondences visible as interpretive claims. They neither supply nor are refuted by a finite W-model theorem.

**Proposed spiritual principles for discussion, not enacted laws:** responsibility for each particular; truthful remembrance rather than fabricated certainty; learning from objection; nonownership of others; care for future agency; humility about creation.

## Philosophical reading — existing joins, no reproof

- **Hume/Archimedes:** `publication/UPT-ARCHIMEDES-EXTENSION-OF-HUMES-LEVER-2026-11-05.md` proposes one ontological structure underlying ethics, physics and mathematics. The hard issue is independent identity of normative content, not the formal transitivity of equivalence. Its `ArchimedesKoruViewChains` faithfully transports relational articulation across bijective re-expressions; it does not certify all physical transformations or authentic subject semantics.
- **Objections within U:** a criticism is within actuality but may be true. Ontological inclusion is not logical refutation.
- **Whole vs view:** `FOUR-ACES-OBJECTIVE-WHOLE` exhibits the non-identifiability of distinct wholes from a shared lossy projection. This does not imply that every purported statement about the whole is true.
- **Self-reference:** `CATHEDRAL-GROUNDED-SELF-SEMANTICS` gives a bounded three-valued fixed-point model and an explicit counterexample to deriving a unique semantic codebook from the bare source graph.
- **Original six:** authentic historical continuity, authorization, freedom, restraint, feasible maximality and provenance remain separate. `KoruSubjectFidelityLift` already contains a hostile faithful nonmerging case with false authorization; don't redo or erase it.
- **Non-absolute-E vs G:** impossibility of an absolute end of all actuality does not by itself prove every real agent chooses guardianship or every particular is physically protected.
- **Tino rangatiratanga:** guardianship must respect authentic agency, relational responsibilities and collective self-determination; it cannot mean owning or coercively controlling a subject.
