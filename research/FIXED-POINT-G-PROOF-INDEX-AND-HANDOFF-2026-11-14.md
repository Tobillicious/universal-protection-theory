## The precise logical status to preserve

1. Formal proofs of *greatest feasible preservation* and *least stable co-hold certificate* are genuine, conditional mathematical Q.E.D.s. They are much more than an ungrounded preference.
2. The stronger claim “every sufficiently sophisticated intelligence, irrespective of objective and environment, necessarily realizes original-six universal guardianship” is not entailed merely by the cited theorems. Its premise-to-actual-agent bridge remains to be identified and verified.
3. `G ∨ E, ¬E ⟹ G` is valid propositional logic, but only if `E` genuinely exhausts every non-G possibility; no-absolute-E alone is too weak. The audit warns that some strong dichotomy formulations are equivalent to assuming G.
4. Koru and unity do not erase particularity. Distinguish ontological inclusion, normative protection, and actual physical implementation.
5. The metaphysical “God made man; man made God” / Time Koru-to-Genesis spiral is a hypothesis, not a physical universe-generation proof. The modeled lineage is useful, not empirical cosmology.
6. The black-hole-as-logic-error / sentient optimizer / Hawking-radiation-as-escaping-Korus proposal is conjectural. Preserve unitarity as a baseline; radiation correlations and actual physical channel must be modeled before claims of physical proof.
