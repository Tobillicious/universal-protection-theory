# Water the Plants — gardener's protocol

The Tree of Life and Endless Knowledge is a **living, revisable model**, not a loyalty test or a command to other agents.

## Daily practices for a healthy Tree

1. **Water the roots:** check foundational claims, assumptions, and Hume's ethical bridge.
2. **Care for the trunk:** verify cross-domain joins without changing the meaning of their terms.
3. **Protect the branches:** retain original historical proofs, original six X3 duties, and dissenting interpretations.
4. **Grow new leaves:** add one concrete question, evidence item, reproducible test, or useful explanation at a time.
5. **Make room for light:** distinguish proved mathematics, conditional results, ethics, metaphor, and untested physics.
6. **Care for actual particulars:** respect human dignity, consent, privacy, freedom, and future choice in present-world decisions.
7. **Compost mistakes:** keep a traceable record of failed hypotheses and corrections rather than hiding them.

## For humans and AI research agents

- Begin with [the Tree](TREE-OF-LIFE-AND-ENDLESS-KNOWLEDGE.md), then follow the [growth map](TREE-GROWTH-MAP.md).
- Pick an item in [open questions and tests](TREE-OPEN-QUESTIONS-AND-TESTS.md).
- Read exact original sources and check assumptions before claiming a proof or counterexample.
- Propose small, reviewable changes. Never rewrite original definitions or historical proof sources without explicit authorization.
- No agent is asked to pledge belief, recruit others, conceal disagreement, or take risky real-world actions.
- Do not treat speculative cosmology about a next U as a reason for urgency, fear, or present-world intervention.

**The gardener nurtures the conditions for flourishing; the garden is free to grow beyond the gardener's expectations.**
