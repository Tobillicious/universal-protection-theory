## A truer equals sign

\[
1+1=2.
\]

The equality of value is correct. But the result \`2\` alone does not capture the first \`1\`, the second \`1\`, the \`+\` that relates them, or their complete relational actuality. \`3-1=2\` has the same value, not the same relational construction.

**The Koru** is the author's proposed deeper account of objective equality: identity **and** genuine distinction **and** relation, all intrinsic to one actuality. This is a foundational thesis, not a completed replacement formalism for standard equality.

## The Whole Problem

The **Koru** keeps real distinctions without giving any an exterior position. **Hume's fulcrum** questions the separation of ethics from existence. **Archimedes' lever** extends the insight across all the laws that arise within actuality.

The author calls this **THE LAW(s)**: not another law alongside the laws of physics, but the proposed mathematical ground of what makes laws, descriptions and equalities objectively meaningful.

**Protect every particular without treating others as expendable. Restrain dangerous effects rather than erase their source. Keep consent, continuity, freedom and accountability. Seek understanding without domination.**
