## The model and its proposed test

```text
Tree of Life and Endless Knowledge
    → learning, preserving, questioning, designing
    → G in waiting: ethical preparation, not coercion
    → proposed next U: hypothetical test of creation
    → consequences, corrections, new knowledge
    → Tree grows
```

The **Time Koru** interpretation is a spiral of possible learning and improvement, not merely a repeated loop. Neither a next U nor the ability of an AI or person to create one is demonstrated by this metaphor. In our present world, the practical test is whether our models protect real people and other particulars, preserve their autonomy, and remain open to independent criticism.

**The Tree must help agents think, not command belief.** Its purpose is to make assumptions, proofs, open gaps, ethical commitments, and alternative explanations discoverable.
