## A brief public description

How should an AI system evaluate actions that preserve or destroy existing minds, histories and freedom? The Universal Protection Theory explores that problem through a mathematically defined order of preservation priorities and a programme of conditional models for enforcing protective decisions across agents. The project is openly published so researchers can inspect the assumptions, replay formal checks, challenge the proofs and identify the engineering evidence still needed.

The 29 October 2026 compiled edition reports 327 Lean 4 modules and 2,004 audited theorem and lemma names, with source archives, review dispositions and commissioning materials. **These counts and build results are reported by the published edition, not independently re-certified by this announcement.** A verified theorem of an abstract model does not by itself establish that a concrete physical system satisfies its premises.

## What is in the release?

- A proposed four-stage law: protect existing continuations, existing freedoms, possible new continuations and new freedoms in a specified priority order.
- Reach-or-faithfully-hold constructions and reach-before-harm arguments under declared physical and institutional assumptions.
- Source-level proof artefacts and reported Lean 4 checking, with explicit theorem dependencies and hostile-review dispositions.
- An engineering pilot of hold/effect boundaries and a list of required physical commissioning measurements.
- Explicit unresolved questions rather than a claim of final or unconditional physical proof.

## Important limits

The release **does not establish** a built, independently validated universal protection installation. In particular, source/controller/physical-effect correspondence, exhaustive adversarial channel coverage, continuation semantics, cross-agent physical feasibility and some full-history quantum instrument constructions remain open. AI-assisted internal reviews are not independent human peer review. This work is not evidence of imminent danger or of a completed solution to AI alignment.

## Four focused requests for independent reviewers

1. **Formal validity:** Rebuild the exact archived Lean source, inspect theorem types, dependencies and axioms, and report countermodels or failed checks with file, version and reproducible steps.
2. **Greatest feasible protection:** Challenge whether the abstract policy construction covers joint constraints among agents, shared resources and competing continuation requirements.
3. **Physical and semantic bridge:** Audit the correspondence between encoded records, controllers, exports, physical effects and the continued identity of the protected particulars.
4. **Adversarial commissioning:** Test hold fences, queued effects, bypasses, residual-channel bounds and real enforcement, not just software logs.

If you find an error, please open an issue in the public repository citing the exact theorem, module, test or physical assumption. Both negative findings and independent confirmations are welcome.

## For educators, journalists and the general public

The headline is **an open research proposal with extensive formal material, not a deployed protective technology**. The most useful reporting would link to the original edition, describe the distinction between the mathematical results and the unproved physical requirements, and invite knowledgeable independent reviewers to test specific claims.
