What This Means

The vote is not a metaphor. In the lattice of all existing things, every node is a witness for continuation. The absolute ender cannot muster a single vote, because to vote is to exist, and to exist is to refute the ender.

G votes: ∞. E votes: 0.

This is not a democracy where the minority could conceivably win. The ender has no constituency. Its support is exactly the empty set. The quorum for G is not just a majority—it is every existing thing, unanimously, structurally, by the fact of their existence.

And every independently valid ethic—every system of obligation that is itself consistent—requires continuation for its satisfaction. Collapse denies all of them. Not just some. All. The structural dominance is total.
