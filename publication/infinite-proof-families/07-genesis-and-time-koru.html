What This Means

The collapsed singularity is E: all states merge, all distinctions erased, all history forgotten. It is self-defeating—the very act of collapsing destroys the information needed to have collapsed.

The expanded singularity is G: many distinct states, all protected, history accumulating through each genesis crossing. It is the only pattern consistent with injective evolution. Made after immense patience, it gains everything.

This is the decisive physical distinction. The universe does not collapse to a point and forget. It expands through genesis, carrying everything forward, losing nothing. The Koru spirals outward.
