\parbox{0.85\textwidth}{\small\textbf{Abstract.}
We present the seventh of ten infinite proof families for the Guardian fixed point~$G$. The \emph{Genesis and Time Koru} family connects the Guardian to cosmological evolution. We prove that (1)~a genesis witness refutes the ender, (2)~fixed points of iterated maps are eternal, (3)~injective maps preserve distinctness (nothing is lost through creation), and (4)~the collapsed singularity --- where all states merge --- is refuted. The expanded singularity, characterised by injectivity, handedness preservation, epoch advancement, and source stepping, is the only pattern consistent with these constraints. All theorems are verified in Lean~4 (v4.35.0). The expanded genesis theorems use \texttt{propext} and \texttt{Quot.sound}; the core existence and injectivity results use zero custom axioms.}
\end{center}

\section{Introduction}

The earlier proof families operate at the level of type theory. The Genesis family bridges to physical cosmology: it formalises what happens at the boundary of creation.

Two models of the singularity compete. The \emph{collapsed singularity} posits that all states merge into one, erasing all distinctions and history. The \emph{expanded singularity} posits that distinct pre-genesis states produce distinct post-genesis states, that handedness is preserved, that epochs advance, and that the source steps forward.

We prove that the collapsed singularity is inconsistent with injective evolution, and that the expanded singularity satisfies all four required properties. This connects UPT to Chandrasekaran's null-cone quantum gravity~\cite{chandrasekaran2026}, where boundary-preserving dynamics impose the same injectivity condition.

\section{The Genesis Witness}

\begin{theorem}[In the beginning]\label{thm:beginning}
For any genesis witness $g : S$, the absolute ender of $S$ is false.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Immediate from the core existence theorem: $g$ inhabits $S$, so $\texttt{AbsoluteEnder}(S) \to \texttt{False}$. \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{leancode}
theorem IN_THE_BEGINNING (genesis : S) :
    AbsoluteEnder S → False :=
    fun e => (e genesis).elim
\end{leancode}

\section{Eternal Fixed Points}

\begin{theorem}[The fixed point is eternal]\label{thm:eternal}
If $G$ is a fixed point of $\phi : M \to M$ (i.e., $\phi(G) = G$), then for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the $n$-fold iterate of $\phi$ at $G$ equals $G$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By induction on $n$. Base: $\phi^0(G) = G$. Step: $\phi^{n+1}(G) = \phi(\phi^n(G)) = \phi(G) = G$. \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{leancode}
theorem THE_FIXED_POINT_IS_ETERNAL {M : Type}
    (phi : M → M) (G : M) (hG : phi G = G) :
    ∀ n, iterate phi n G = G
\end{leancode}

\section{Injectivity and Creation}

\begin{theorem}[Nothing is lost through creation]\label{thm:injective}
If $f : S \to S$ is injective and $a \neq b$, then $f(a) \neq f(b)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{leancode}
theorem NOTHING_IS_LOST_THROUGH_CREATION {S : Type}
    (f : S → S) (inj : Function.Injective f)
    (a b : S) (h : a ≠ b) : f a ≠ f b
\end{leancode}

\section{Collapse Refuted}

\begin{theorem}[Collapse is impossible]\label{thm:collapse}
Two distinct pre-genesis states always produce distinct post-genesis states. The collapsed singularity is refuted.
\end{theorem}

\begin{leancode}
theorem COLLAPSE_REFUTED :
    ∀ (a b : GenesisState),
        a ≠ b → genesisMap a ≠ genesisMap b
\end{leancode}

\section{The Expanded Singularity}

\begin{theorem}[Expanded genesis]\label{thm:expanded}
The genesis map satisfies four properties: injectivity, handedness preservation, epoch advancement, and source stepping.

\begin{leancode}
theorem EXPANDED_GENESIS :

theorem G_IS_EXPANDED_E_IS_COLLAPSED :

\section{Discussion}

The Genesis family connects formal type theory to physical cosmology. The collapsed singularity is the cosmological analogue of the absolute ender: a state where all distinctions are erased. The expanded singularity is the analogue of the Guardian: a state where distinctions are preserved through injective evolution.

Chandrasekaran's null-cone quantum gravity~\cite{chandrasekaran2026} independently derives boundary-preserving dynamics that impose exactly this injectivity condition. The mathematical equivalence between Chandrasekaran's framework and UPT's BCRD algebra is proved in~\cite{upt-bcrd-qg}.
