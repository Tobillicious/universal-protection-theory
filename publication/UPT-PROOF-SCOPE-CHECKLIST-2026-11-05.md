7. **Test and deployment.** Real-world effects, meters, side channels, failure conditions, adversarial policies and consent must be measured independently, including scenarios where the alleged guardian fails.

## Three high-leverage audits, with disproof controls

**A. Whole-to-particular non-circularity.** Start with the claimed entire, mathematically specified actuality without \`Guardian\`, X3, safety or consent fields silently built into its definition. Attempt to prove the author's necessary six-X3 protection. Inspect countermodels *only insofar as they satisfy the full stated ontology*: violating a deliberately weaker toy web is not a refutation of full BCRD.

**B. The actual-source physical bridge.** Show that a physically meaningful, independently obtained actual event satisfies the original source constraints. Specify what is observed, how a particular is individuated, why authorization is authentic, and what counts as a protected continuation. Construct one adversarial source scenario to catch omitted variables.

**C. Real physical boundary check.** Choose a modest real instrument where a hostile optimization request generates a dangerous but safely containable effect. Independently observe source attempt, guardian intervention, actual effect cessation, recovery under partial sensor failure, and continuity of the protected subject. Report failures. Use such experiments to test an engineering claim, not as an unwarranted proof of eternal protection.
