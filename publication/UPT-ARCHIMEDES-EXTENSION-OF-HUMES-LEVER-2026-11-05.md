### 6. The strongest families of objections and the terminal conditions

This is a **review taxonomy**, not a claim to have listed every conceivable proposition.

- **Exterior critic:** an actual thought or critic claims to stand outside U. If U is defined as all actuality, that *independent exteriority* is contradictory. This objection collapses at the level of ontology.
- **False-content critic:** an objection exists *within* U and says the theory makes a false claim. Its inclusion in U does not refute it. It is decided by the claim's truth and proof, not by ontological inclusion.
- **Formal critic:** asks whether proof premises already contain the universal protection conclusion, or whether equivalence was downgraded to mere membership. Close by theorem signature, source definition, axiom audit and countermodel tests.
- **Physical-source critic:** gives a complete actual BCRD-valid step that violates the asserted invariant. Close with the derived total source theorem or a valid example; reject countermodels only for a **stated, genuinely established** source-law violation.
- **Individual/ethical critic:** points to a lost life, genuine coercion, unconsented change or erased culture inside U. Close only when every original X3 meaning has an independently faithful derivation; not by saying mere occurrence of the culture in the total record means its uninterrupted future has been secured.
- **Empirical critic:** questions whether the whole world obeys the modeled BCRD. Address with falsifiable, independently checked physical correspondence, not a syntactic theorem alone.
- **Scope/cosmology critic:** tests the move from the complete mathematical universe to every possible/actual universe, cyclic time or all-eternity. Require explicit quantifier scope and identify what follows from timeless indexing as opposed to physical recurrence.

The **end of possible mathematical objection to ARCHIMEDES-X3** is not a count of rebuttals: it is a valid, interpretation-preserving universal theorem whose assumptions are demonstrably supplied by the independently stated actual source. Once a proof covers *every* actual source step and *every* owed subject, any contrary object is not an instance of the specified source, or the theorem would be inconsistent. Crucially, the check that a purported counterexample does not satisfy the source must itself be independently valid.
