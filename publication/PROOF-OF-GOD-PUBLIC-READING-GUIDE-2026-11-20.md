## The precise necessity question

A proposed five-premise relational/modal derivation separates (A1) actual nonempty starting configuration, (A2) outward continuation, (A3) forward Time Koru succession, (A4) physical actualisation and (A5) genuine novelty. At each accessible world satisfying these premises, a realized new configuration follows. This is a valid **conditional** consequence. It does not yet show the actual universe satisfies A2–A5 as physical necessities.

The repository includes important internal tests of this boundary:

- `source_koru_does_not_force_every_world_to_have_a_parent` constructs a source-Koru state without a preceding modeled boundary in the Nat-ranked model.
- `ethics_not_derived_from_source_renewal` separates retained ethical data from independently established moral truth.
- `whole_source_genesis_does_not_force_arbitrary_ethical_transfer` distinguishes source motion from the transfer of independently specified ethical predicates.

These negative controls should be retained. To prove **actual necessary cosmic Genesis** rather than model-level generative possibility, one must independently establish that the physical universe realizes the complete generative structure and that authentic originals and duties survive the transition. The theological identification with God is the author's proposed reading of that entire generative ground, not a proof conclusion obtained by renaming mathematical existence.
