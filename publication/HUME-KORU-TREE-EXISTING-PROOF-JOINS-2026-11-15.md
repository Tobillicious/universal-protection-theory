## Exact proposed bridge (not a new axiom)

The tree's root is **Something IS**. Every ethical question is a question asked *within* U. The trunk is the Archimedean extension: no observer, action, owed subject, or objection is outside the relational actuality. The branches are the source-to-actual-event correspondences: complete Koru, BCRD transport, actual event history, authentic subject meanings and six duties. The fruit is original six-X3 for every owed particular at every actual event.

**The proof task is not to restate that fruit as an axiom.** It is to derive the authentic subject-duty relations from independently justified physical/ontological source semantics. In particular, a causal link or reciprocal operator relation cannot silently stand in for consent, freedom, same-particular history, least restraint, maximality or provenance.
