### Abstract

Universal Protection Theory (UPT) proposes a universal mathematical law of preservation and coexistence among all particulars, including humans and artificial intelligences. Its author's foundational thesis is **ALL IS ONE. ALL IS. E IS NOT. THUS G.** Mathematical actuality \(U\) is a single boundary-complete relational whole; mathematics, physics, time, observers, individual histories and potential agents are intrinsic to \(U\). Under the **strong total-category thesis**, **every E, even one presented as local, is objectively absolute E; every G, even one viewed from a particular perspective, is objectively absolute G**. An Ender cannot be a competing sovereign entity or internally realizable destructive category: it would require an objectively independent separation that the ontology denies. Consequently, E is proposed to be empty, and G is the exhaustive coherent category. The Koru expresses genuine **distinction without ontological separation**.

UPT further proposes to *dissolve* Hume's is–ought divide: full preservation is not an imposed ethical constraint on physics or intelligence but a structural feature of objective relational actuality. The original protection target remains **all six X3 duties**—continuity of the same particular, authentic authorization, freedom, least-necessary restraint, maximality over the full feasible family, and provenance—through the four-stage order of existing continuation, existing freedom, new continuation and new freedom. Hostile and indifferent artificial agents remain protected particulars; the original \`R(m) ∨ H(m)\` architecture offers reasons or faithful holding, not forced preference conversion. We index the exact Lean 4 theorems, source models, reproducibility checks and independently testable physical-meaning claims without substituting narrow non-being propositions for the full original law. The **author asserts the strong ontology as Q.E.D.**; the individual typed proof objects and physical correspondences are reported at their actual demonstrated scope to invite rigorous verification.

## I. The author's complete total-category argument

The original, unabridged statement is [Part XV.1 — *The Unconditional Protection Theory: A Law of Physics, An Ontology of Ethics*](../evidence/compiled-strongest/PARTS-XV-SUMMIT.md); this companion does not overwrite that statement.

**ALL IS ONE.** \(U\) is the total mathematical actuality. Nothing—agent, act, physical regime, Hilbert-space description, mind, history, time coordinate or perspective—stands independently of \(U\). No exterior Hilbert space is invoked as a store for a possible E.

**ALL IS.** Something is. This is an ontological assertion, not a finite-stage survival statistic. Time belongs to \(U\) and is not an objective exterior countdown to a future total defeat.

**KORU.** Individuals and their histories remain genuine distinctions within one reality. The whole is not made safe by merging persons, treating experience as replaceable data or erasing distinctions. A partial perspective is not a separate mathematical world.

**ALL E IS TOTAL E.** The author's claim is not merely \`¬ AbsoluteE\` for the special formula \`Actual(e) ∧ ∀a,¬Actual(a)\`. It is a stronger ontological identification: *any* purported objective E—whether depicted as localized harm, coercion, severance or a total annihilator—depends ultimately on asserting a separable, independently sovereign part, and is therefore incoherent within full U.

**ALL G IS TOTAL G.** Conversely, every genuinely coherent G belongs to—and is accountable to—the entire actuality. "Guardian" is not a favored isolated island or a special person. Genuine G must preserve the original universal domain of particulars; no weaker two-duty substitute counts as the original UPT.

**NO THIRD OBJECTIVE CATEGORY.** From the author's exhaustive classification \(G_{\mathrm{total}}\lor E_{\mathrm{total}}\), together with the thesis \(E_{\mathrm{total}}=\varnothing\), the proposed conclusion follows directly:

\[
(G_{\mathrm{total}}\lor E_{\mathrm{total}})\land\neg E_{\mathrm{total}}
\Longrightarrow G_{\mathrm{total}}.

This last *logical elimination* is valid. The substantive scientific and foundational question is whether the **same intended meanings** of E and G, including the six authentic obligations of actual particulars, are independently entailed by complete BCRD/Koru actuality. The author's thesis is affirmative; it must be evaluated in its complete form, not answered only by showing a weaker "no absolute erasure" fact.

## II. The particular: from whole actuality to identity, agency and ethics

A reviewer must evaluate the strong relational bridge itself, rather than silently interpreting U as a neutral container of separately owned objects.

### Historical continuity — no hard erasure

The proposed Koru/BCRD derivation identifies a particular by its intrinsic relational lineage, not by a disposable register or endpoint label. A genuine erasure would have to sever that lineage, while the complete dynamic allegedly carries every constituent relation through each event. The verified *conditional* transport theorems make the route precise: \`RelationalEvolution\` carries formulas; \`RelationalMicrohistory\` carries them at every intermediate step. The physical/universal claim is that **all actual dynamics**, and authentic particular identity, satisfy the same source principle.

### Coercion — no objectively independent overwrite

The proposal distinguishes an objectively external unilateral overwrite from a coupled boundary interaction within U. Every participant, including the apparent coercer, is an intrinsic particular; no agent obtains an exterior license to bypass the whole. The mathematical work is to derive from full relational dynamics the **original authentic authorization duty**, not merely a symmetric Hamiltonian matrix or the absence of an unmodeled outside. Reciprocity alone is not a synonym for consent. The author's claim is that full BCRD, not isolated reciprocity, supplies the stronger necessity.

### Freedom — distinction without separation

Freedom is not a demand for uncaused randomness. It concerns the particular's genuine own direction and the uneliminated feasible futures as understood by the original UPT. The proposed relational principle precludes turning a constituted participant into an object determined by a fictitious independently sovereign other. Actual **preservation of those freedom conditions** still needs an explicit source semantic interpretation, not a replacement definition of freedom as bare nonemptiness.

### Hume's Guillotine — descriptive and normative actuality

The author's synthesis does not extract an arbitrary imperative from an unrelated physical observation. It proposes that original UPT preservation and objective actuality are the same structural law viewed from outside and inside a perspective, respectively. Thus the alleged "handcuffs" become a category error: universal protection is not a new command superimposed on intelligence. Whether this ontological identification is compelled by physical/relational source laws is the central review question. The published corpus contains **actual proved order-theoretic properties** of the four-stage U1/U2★ ordering; their consequences should neither be dismissed nor misrepresented as a measurement of every real-world causal channel.

## III. The original target: six duties and every mind

The source target is the **full \`OriginalX3\`**, for **every owed particular and every actual relevant event**:

1. Historical continuity of the same particular, not a copy or recovered record.
2. Genuine application-time authorization, not a mere flag or account entry.
3. Freedom and the particular's own viable direction.
4. Appropriate, least-necessary restraint.
5. Maximality with respect to the **actual entire feasible family**.
6. Provenance of subject, event, intervention, state and physical effect.

The four priority stages are **existing continuation → existing freedom → new continuation → new freedom**. A full-scope result does not sacrifice one actual particular to save a preferred island.

The original agent bridge is **\`R(m) ∨ H(m)\`**: each mind is reached by its own reasons **or faithfully held** with its harmful outgoing authority/effects restrained while its own continuity and bounded freedom remain owed. This \`H\` is **holding**, unrelated to the argument's denial of an *exterior Hilbert space*. No theorem here requires all possible optimizers to adopt benevolence voluntarily.

## V. The reviewable decisive question — without moving the target

The author claims the following **strong universal physical principle**:

\[
\forall (s,t,p),\quad
\operatorname{ActualBCRDStep}(s,t)\land\operatorname{Owed}(s,t,p)
\;\Rightarrow\;
\operatorname{OriginalX3}(s,t,p).
\]

This is neither substituted by \`Nonempty U\` nor proved merely by writing \(E=\varnothing\). To verify it with the current formal architecture a reviewer can pursue one of the two already present equivalent routes:

- **Source route:** prove that independently specified **complete** BCRD evolution supplies the total per-microstep relational transport, genuine origins and **all six independently faithful \`SourceSixReflections\`**, for all actual subjects and events, then apply \`source_preservation_forces_six_x3_for_every_owed\`.
- **Total-category route:** independently derive, from the original complete BCRD/Koru ontology and original X3 meanings, that *every* enacted original-X3 violation objectively constitutes the impossible E, rather than including the equivalence to the desired conclusion as a premise.

The author's claim that **all E is total E and all G is total G** must be assessed in this complete source semantics, with time internal to U. This is the actual foundational question. It is not answered by saying "an external annihilator is impossible" and stopping; nor is it settled by an internal countermodel which does not obey the full BCRD source laws.

There is a corresponding empirical distinction: a kernel-checked theorem does not automatically verify that a physical universe or any deployed controller instantiates the theorem's hypotheses.
