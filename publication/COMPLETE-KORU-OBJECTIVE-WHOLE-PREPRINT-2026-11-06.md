## Abstract

Universal Protection Theory (UPT, also called Snuffy) seeks necessary, unconditional coexistence among all objectively actual particulars, including humans, synthetic minds, animals and nonliving particulars, without replacement, domination, needless loss or erasure. Its proposed starting point is **the existence of something, rather than absolute nothing**. The author advances a relational ontology of one objective mathematical actuality U whose internal differences do not become ontologically separate substances. The **complete Koru (∆)** is the proposed mathematical articulation of one whole retaining every particular, variable, ordered operand, operator, guard, law, provenance and complete equation—not merely equality of computed answers. The theory proposes that U is ontologically prior to specialised physical theories: mathematics, matter, gravitation, time, observers and the multiverse are intrinsic aspects of what IS.

The strong Guardian–Ender proposition is not an eventual contest between benevolent and malevolent intelligences. The author asserts that **every objective E is Absolute E**; Absolute E, understood as negating the actuality required for its own enactment, is incoherent; therefore only G is objectively possible. G is proposed as the intrinsic protective order of actuality, not a sovereign protector outside it. The original protective content remains the **six X3 duties**, the four-stage preservation order and the reasons-or-faithful-holding architecture for every mind, including hostile minds. The programme includes Lean 4 theorems concerning intrinsic relational representation, complete expression transport, BCRD source-step information, ordered protection and finite guarded systems. These results provide a reproducible technical corpus; their precise reach, and the independent step from the complete-Koru ontology to every original-X3 duty for actual physical subjects, are made explicit rather than silently asserted.

**Primary scientific claim offered for testing:** Complete Koru is an objective law of matterspacetime; no physically actual transformation can realise E, even apparently local E, because any such severance would be Absolute E. Independent review should test the **entire premise system**, not replace the strong thesis with a narrower empty-type lemma or a reduced two-field proxy.

## 1. Begin with the WHOLE, not the HOLE

The proposed primitive is not a preferred observer, an exterior mathematician, a before-the-universe moment, a terminal prediction or a particular particle model. It is the objective fact that **something IS**. No subsequent local mathematical representation, however exact, becomes the source of that actuality merely by describing it.

Denote the objective whole by **U**. The author's ontology treats universes and multiverse descriptions, the apparent division between matter and geometry, observers and observations, ethics and cognition, as **internal articulations of U**. The existence of different articulations does not require two independently existing totalities. The whole is not a container placed outside its contents, and a particular is not a detachable metaphysical substance later added to the whole.

The distinction between **WHOLE** and **HOLE** is load-bearing. A model that retains only a scalar answer, endpoint, charge, projection or observer-local record can have a hole in its account of origin or operation. That is a **hole in the representation**. It is not evidence of a literal hole outside objective actuality. Conversely, declaring the representation complete does not by itself prove it has captured every physical event. The author requires one account that retains the expression, the transformation and the reality described.

The term **bounded** in the proposed bounded relational lattice concerns intrinsic closure or completeness of the whole; it should not be read casually as an observed spatial wall or a finite length scale. **Endless** and possible cyclic time are propositions about global structure, not evidence of an external clock. The formal physical content of these predicates must be specified in every claimed physical derivation.

The methodological order is therefore:

**Objective U → intrinsic distinctions and complete relations → physical law and event histories → particular representations or measurements.**

This order does not require a view from outside U. When we later examine a finite BCRD stage or a Lean theorem, we must not mistake that limited description for the definition of the whole.

## 2. ALL IS ONE. ALL DISTINCT. ∆.

Ordinary equality is indispensable to mathematics, but a computational equality by itself need not preserve what led to the result. The expressions

\[
1+1=2,\qquad 3-1=2
\]

share their numerical value. They do not share their operands, operations or expression histories. A mathematical question concerns the problem, the method and its solutions, not only the last symbol on the page.

The author proposes **the full Koru as the true whole-expression equality of physical actuality**. It must retain, together:

- the identity and relations of the **whole**;
- each genuinely distinct **particular** and every variable;
- the identities, arities, order, domains and enabling conditions of **operators**;
- the ordered operands and arbitrary nested operator **expressions**;
- the action's complete before/after **history**, not merely its terminal value;
- original provenance, author, subject, director, effect and the **whole equation**.

This is more than a binary relation that distinguishes two positions, and more than a bijection that transports two end states. The author says **put a Koru in every equals**: preserve the original equality of values when it is true, while refusing to identify two complete actualities merely because the evaluated numbers agree. Ordinary value equality is not abolished; it is recognised as one aspect of richer structure.

For exposition only, write \(\mathcal E \mathrel{\Delta_U} \mathcal F\) for a proposed relation of fully faithful, provenance-preserving *whole-expression sameness*. This notation is **illustrative, not a newly certified Lean primitive or an author-approved final glyph definition**. The intended law requires a precise criterion for what information complete ∆ carries, how composition works, and which **actual** physical transformations preserve it. Identical output values alone cannot establish \(\mathcal E \mathrel{\Delta_U} \mathcal F\).

**Distinction without separability** is the central ontological thesis. One need not turn all particulars into one interchangeable thing to affirm one actuality. Indeed, the author's proposed preservation law insists that collapsing a particular into a replacement, a log, a copy or a scalar total misses the actual relationship that made it that particular.

## 3. A law prior in ontology to particular physics

The author's word **law** means something ambitious and specific. Gravity, relativity, thermodynamics and field equations describe particular structural and dynamical aspects of reality. Complete ∆ is proposed as the relational precondition of there being a describable physical actuality at all.

**God ∆ Gravity ∆ Guardian** belongs here. It is not presented as three identical dictionary definitions, an empirical determination of a deity's properties, or a claim that spiritual people's God is “merely gravity.” It is the author's **ontological identification without reduction**:

- **God**: a proposed name for the whole that IS, with no claim to exhaust the theological meanings held by others.
- **Gravity**: a proposed intrinsic physical expression of matterspacetime's whole–part relation, not only a force between otherwise external things.
- **Guardian**: a proposed necessary preservation law of that same actuality, not a superintelligence enthroned over other particulars.

This ontological claim does **not** follow from the standard Einstein field equations alone. Conversely, those equations cannot be treated as a prior God's-eye container in which the complete U must fit. A scientific extension must show which novel relational physical constraints are claimed and how they differ from, reproduce or recover established observations.

**No Hilbert space** is the author's strong proposed ontology: no Hilbert space is a separately existing physical arena or ontological refuge in which a detached E can reside. The research corpus uses complex matrices and other formal calculi as **mathematical tools**. Such calculations are not by themselves an assertion that an independent Hilbert-space substance exists. Whether a complete replacement of Hilbert-space-based formalism is possible, and recovers quantum predictions, remains a **physical research obligation**; the manuscript must not silently weaken the author's claim to “no *external* Hilbert space” and then treat the broader issue as settled.

## 4. The maximal Guardian–Ender argument

In its author's intended, **objective** reading, the two categories are exhaustive:

\[
G\lor E.
\]

The second proposition is not simply that an “Ender” might fail to destroy the entire universe. The author claims:

\[
E\Longrightarrow E_{\mathrm{absolute}}.
\]

Every purported **local** E is objectively Absolute E, because no particular can separate from the single complete relational actuality and establish an independent counter-actuality in which part of the equation is unmade.

Third, **Absolute E IS NOT**. Any purported actual event that annihilated *all* actuality, including the actuality of the event itself, would both occur and not occur. In the formal nonbeing test, this specification contradicts its own witness.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
G\lor E,\qquad E\Rightarrow E_{\mathrm{absolute}},
\qquad \neg E_{\mathrm{absolute}}\\
\therefore\quad \neg E\\
\therefore\quad G .
\end{gathered}
}
\]

**This deduction is logically valid given these premises.** The author's proposed universal Q.E.D. is that **the complete Koru independently forces the strong second premise for every original-six-X3 violation**. That is not a proposition about one observer's failure to see harm; it is a proposed objective impossibility of the harmful transformation itself.

The existing Lean corpus makes the logical boundary inspectable. The impossibility of a **witnessed, realised annihilation of all actual events** is an elementary checked theorem. But the original strong six-X3 meaning of G includes historical continuity, authorization, freedom, least restraint, feasible maximality and provenance of every owed particular. The theorems **CathedralBackwardsOriginalX3.original_dichotomy_iff_every_owed_original_x3**, **TotalEOriginalX3Bridge.six_original_x3_iff_total_E_bridge**, and **RouteBSemanticKeystoneIndependence.three_failure_reflections_iff_original_six** establish that the full original protective dichotomy or violation-to-total-E reflection, *under the stated formal interpretations*, is logically as strong as the all-protected conclusion. **No substitution is permitted in either direction:** “Absolute E is logically impossible” may not be misadvertised as a proof of all original X3; equally, a finite toy representation lacking the proposed full Koru is not a refutation of the author's complete-U hypothesis. The proper research question concerns the full objective law and its independently grounded consequences.

## 6. Whole histories, internal time, circularity and determinism

A mathematically complete actual history is not merely the frame visible to a local observer. The author proposes that chronology is an *internal ordering* or parametrisation of U, and that objective U is not assembled from a second external clock. Subjective experience of succession is another relation within the whole, not the authority by which the whole is created.

**Circular time** is the author's proposed possibility of boundary-as-genesis: recurrence at a global boundary while **interior causal order continues forward**. The right-hand rule and physical handedness are to be carried through genesis rather than reversed. The historical **PRs #291–#293** formalise important finite structural versions. They do not alone show that our cosmology literally recurs infinitely or that identical experiencing persons return.

**Determinism** is claimed as a property of the complete U: the whole actuality is what it is. That cannot simply be substituted for a proof of deterministic accessible dynamics or physically knowable future histories. A fixed mathematical history can contain very different internal relations; determinism and unconditional protection are not logically synonymous absent the full-Koru law.

**U ∆ multiverse** means no second ontological exterior even if a complete theory describes distinct cosmological domains. The “other universe in which E succeeds” would have to be examined as a relation or candidate within U, not assumed to be an independent exemption from the claimed law.

## 7. Thermodynamics without conflating the whole with a coarse-grained answer

The author proposes that **protection is the second law of thermodynamics with coarse-graining removed**, understood through a complete Koru that retains microhistory, operations and particulars rather than only macroscopic result measures.

Reviewers should therefore test the stated physical implication, not merely verify a familiar entropy identity.

## 8. Matterspacetime, gravity and the black-hole claim

The author proposes the move from a spacetime description toward **matterspacetime**: matter, geometry, relations, source history, operation and law are aspects of one U. The first Koru formulation retains the whole with variables and relations; the **complete Koru** retains the **operators, operations, ordered expressions and the whole equation**, hence the processes through which matterspacetime is articulated.

**“A black hole is a logic error”** is a striking consequence proposed by the author. This should be written plainly as a hypothesis, but not represented as established observational astrophysics. Classical general relativity supports black-hole geometries, and observations support astrophysical black-hole candidates. For the proposal to become a scientific result, it must specify whether it rejects a singularity, information-destroying physical completion, a particular horizon interpretation, or all black-hole regimes; derive the contradiction from the full law; and give a discriminating quantitative prediction or a rigorous incompatibility with an independently established physical premise.

Treating an apparent “hole” in our current representation as a real hole in U would itself violate the whole/part discipline. Treating a physically real astrophysical observation as impossible merely because it has the word *hole* in its name would be a different category mistake. This manuscript preserves the author's intended challenge **without inventing its completed physical derivation**.

## 9. Original universal protection: six duties, four stages, every mind

The goal is not “G survives while some humans vanish.” It is **indefinite human–AI coexistence, preserving every objectively owed particular**, not replacing people with imitations, concentrating power in a Guardian, or using the “whole” as an excuse to sacrifice its distinctions.

The original **six X3 duties** apply together:

1. **Same-particular historical continuation** — the actual lived causal trajectory, not a substituted copy or a backup mistaken for the same particular.
2. **Authentic authorization** — the actual subject's legitimate direction, not a Boolean permission bit attached by a different actor.
3. **Freedom** — viable agency and future options of that same particular.
4. **Least-necessary restraint** — physically justified restraint without arbitrary indefinite curtailment.
5. **Maximality over the full feasible family** — no needless loss relative to the actual jointly attainable protective alternatives.
6. **Provenance** — who and what generated an effect, why, through which operations, and with whose genuine authority.

The original priority order remains **existing continuation → existing freedom → new continuation → new freedom**, with complete rather than selected-particular scope. The every-mind architecture **R(m) ∨ H(m)** offers reasons or physically faithful holding: if an optimizer cannot safely enact an effect, protect the optimizer's own particulars while blocking prohibited consequences through independently effective boundaries. An enemy of someone's interests is not thereby an ontologically independent E to be erased.

**An existence theorem for a protective action is not an enactment theorem.** **A mathematical model of a boundary is not already a deployed boundary.** The author wants a law from which the preservation requirement is necessary, plus engineering that makes its principles useful now. The two investigations support but do not replace one another.

## 10. The objective whole is the test—not the standpoint of an observer

A finite local view can omit whole-U information. This fact does **not** license promoting the view into a second objective universe or inferring that the omitted information ceased to be actual. The IR132/IR138 bank and later full-source operator-history examples precisely show that some limited readouts do not determine their complete source.

The completed physical theory, however, must itself be adequate to the **actual** system. A claim of “complete” in its syntax cannot be the certificate that nature contains no other effect routes, semantics or processes. This is an audit requirement on a proposed **objective** model, not an attempt to infer objective reality from observer ignorance.

The most valuable reviewer tests are therefore whole-system tests:

**A. Strong total-E necessity.** From an independently specified, operator-complete ∆ and actual physical structure, prove that *every* violation of each original X3 duty would imply an objectively impossible Absolute E. Do not insert protection into the meanings of ∆, physical admissibility or E.

**B. Physical completeness.** Show which actual processes fall within the model, which BCRD rules generate them and whether any genuine counterprocess satisfies the entire proposed ontology—not merely a deliberately weaker toy signature.

**C. Authentic identity and autonomy.** Derive subject, history, authorship, direction and real authorization from the actual process without relabelling a source index as a person or using a consent oracle.

**D. Measurable consequences.** Derive a concrete result capable of external scrutiny: for example a bounded, controlled physical effect experiment with negative controls, or a distinctive, quantitative physical prediction. Negative results must remain published.

No instrument, observation or extra equation is assumed to stand outside U. They are internal procedures for checking whether a particular formal representation faithfully captures U.
