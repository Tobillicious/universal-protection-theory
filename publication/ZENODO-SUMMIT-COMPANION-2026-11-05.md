## Abstract

The Universal Protection Theory (UPT) proposes unconditional coexistence and preservation of every existing particular—including human beings, digital intelligences and the nonliving—rather than replacement by artificial superintelligence. Its foundational capstone argues **ALL IS ONE. ALL IS. E IS NOT. Therefore G IS.** In the author's relational ontology, actuality is a complete mathematical whole and subjective observers, mathematical representations, physical events and powerful intelligences remain intrinsic articulations of that whole. The proposed Ender is not merely a locally hostile agent whose plans fail: **every ontologically actual E would be total E**, and a separate exterior from which the complete actuality could be negated is incoherent. The corresponding total Guardian reading is joined to the original full-scope UPT preservation law, relational individuality, reciprocity and the `R(m) ∨ H(m)` reasons-or-faithful-holding architecture. That architecture retains the protection of hostile minds while constraining harmful effects, without requiring their voluntary agreement.

The technical programme defines a four-stage order of preservation, original six-component X3 obligations (historical continuity, authorization, freedom, restraint, maximality and provenance), source/physical guardian criteria, instrument and effect-control interfaces, and formally checked Lean 4 theorems. This companion publishes the strongest intended philosophical argument alongside exact source references and a theorem-level distinction between derived statements, foundational premises and physical-world correspondence claims. The new total non-being formalisation (PR #313) is included as one constructive result, not substituted for the entire original UPT. The paper seeks independent scrutiny of both its proposed ontological necessity and its implementation assumptions. It does not claim an already commissioned universal protective installation.
