Do **not** weaken the target. The target is protection of every actual owed particular across every actual event, including within-event transitions, with the original same-particular identity, freedom, authorization, least restraint, maximality and provenance obligations intact.

There are **two distinct routes** to investigate, and neither may be silently assumed:

**A. Physical installation route:** independently establish universal coverage of actual particulars and events; physical identity/readout correspondence; measured upper bounds for all perturbations and crosstalk; realizable correction bandwidth, latency, energy and reliability; initialization and fault accounting; and a guarantee that unaccounted exceptions cannot defeat the universal claim. Finite-horizon probability bounds with nonzero exceptional mass do **not** imply zero-failure infinite-horizon protection. A toy `W=0` witness proves nonvacuity only.

Both routes can contribute; neither is licensed to replace the original theorem by a proxy.
