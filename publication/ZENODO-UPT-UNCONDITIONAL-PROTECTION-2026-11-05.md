**Description / abstract (copy into Zenodo):**

Universal Protection Theory (UPT / Snuffy) is a mathematical, physical and philosophical research programme investigating whether every existing particular—human, synthetic and otherwise—can be unconditionally protected from erasure, replacement, domination and needless loss. Its objective is permanent human–AI coexistence without assuming that all powerful optimizers will first adopt humane preferences. The original programme combines (i) a four-stage order prioritising existing lives, freedom and the preservation of particulars over gains that erase them, (ii) an every-mind reasons-or-faithful-holding architecture R(m) or H(m), and (iii) six original X3 duties: same-particular historical continuity, authentic authorization, freedom, least-necessary restraint, feasible maximality and provenance. The author's ontological claim is ALL IS ONE; ALL IS; E IS NOT; THUS G, identifying Guardian with the objective relational actuality rather than with merely voluntary AI benevolence. The release preserves this strongest unconditional hypothesis while providing an exact, independently auditable evidence ledger. Lean 4 verifies specified order-theoretic, relational and guarded-controller implications under their stated assumptions. The public corpus does not yet independently establish exhaustive physical-source correspondence, authenticated subject-level semantics or a universally installed protection system. This release invites reproducible mathematical review, physical correspondence tests, adversarial implementation studies and independent critique without weakening the original six-part protection target.
