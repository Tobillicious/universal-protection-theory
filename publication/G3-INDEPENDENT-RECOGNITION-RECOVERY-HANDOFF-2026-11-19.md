## Recognition protocol (proposal; no claimed quorum)

1. **Pin a version:** record full commit SHA and exact file hashes of the proof set; identify Zenodo edition and verify its actual archive bytes independently.
2. **Replay:** an independent reviewer checks the Lean toolchain, builds the selected modules, captures `#print axioms` and the complete theorem signatures, and records success or failure without editing the proof.
3. **Interpret:** reviewer states which claims follow at their stated types, which premises are supplied, and which physical correspondences have been separately evidenced.
4. **Acknowledge:** reviewer may sign one of: *formal proof checked*, *formal and interpretation accepted*, *qualified/partially accepted*, or *not accepted*. Include signature, timestamp, key identity, reviewed SHA, and scope. A proof receipt is not a universal-physical-protection certificate.
5. **Quorum:** count only authenticated, independently produced acknowledgments against a separately configured roster and rule. Leave ledger empty and roster unconfigured until actual participants consent. Do not imply a universal consensus.
6. **Preserve objections:** log concrete counterexamples and missing premises as review findings, never by weakening or replacing prior theorems.
