# Universal Protection Theory (UPT / Snuffy): a one-page guide for skeptical readers

**Version-specific entry point:** [UPT compiled edition 4.3 (29 October 2026)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23760830)  
**Public source and review:** [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Tobillicious/universal-protection-theory) · [Issue tracker](https://github.com/Tobillicious/universal-protection-theory/issues)  
**Author:** S. Tobias Croydon-McRae, independent researcher, Aotearoa New Zealand  
**Status:** open research, with author-reported formal checks; **not** an externally validated physical safety system.

## In sixty seconds

An increasingly capable agent can produce an enormous range of outcomes. Which outcomes should it prefer when its actions could end existing beings, interrupt their histories, restrict their freedoms, or replace them with new beings?

UPT proposes a **lexicographic preservation order**: existing continuations first; existing freedoms next; then possible new continuations and new freedoms. It develops order-theoretic results and conditional constructions intended to enforce protective choices, including a *reach-or-faithfully-hold* approach to constrained effect channels.

The latest archive **reports** 327 Lean 4 modules and 2,004 audited theorem/lemma names. A Lean proof can establish that a conclusion follows from formalized premises; it cannot by itself establish that the premises hold for a real physical controller, or that an encoded history constitutes the continuing existence of a particular mind. The archive's build numbers have not been independently re-certified in this note.

**This is not a claim that universal alignment, universal adoption, quantum gravity or a physically complete protection system has been achieved.**

## The useful objection is specific

A skeptical reviewer does not need to read the whole book. A valuable contribution could answer **one** of these five questions:

1. **Formal validity and scope.** Which theorem statement, imported axiom, hidden premise or vacuous case fails to support the published natural-language interpretation? Identify the exact version, Lean module, theorem and minimal countermodel.
2. **Joint feasibility.** Does prioritizing preservation for each agent separately imply the existence of a *single* physically feasible greatest protective outcome under shared resources and interacting constraints? Supply a finite counterexample or proof under explicit conditions.
3. **Source-to-world correspondence.** Can an untrusted resident bypass the software monitor or output fence, submit a forged authorized-looking effect, or achieve a consequential effect through a channel not modelled by the instrumentation? Show an executable trace and the precise trusted boundary.
4. **Continuation semantics.** Which conditions would make a retained record a continuing *particular* mind rather than merely a reconstructible description or replica? Show the bridge from journal/projection to identity, or a counterexample.
5. **Adversarial and physical commissioning.** Are HOLD acknowledgements, queued effects, crashes, bypasses, residual emissions and real enforcement bounded by observed evidence rather than simply assumed? Identify the missing measurement or a breaking test.

A failed test, a well-scoped positive replication, or an explicit 'not established' finding is useful. Please include **what you checked, what you did not check, and how another person could reproduce it**.

## Claim-status map

| Layer | What the published archive offers | What is *not* established by that alone |
|---|---|---|
| Normative ordering | A proposed preservation priority order and its formal consequences | Every agent endorsing or adopting that order |
| Mathematical formalization | Reported Lean-checked propositions and explicit assumptions | Independent reconstruction of every stated consequence |
| Conditional software enforcement | Demonstrations and pilot tests around hold/permit/effect boundaries | All adversarial physical output channels being enclosed |
| Mind/history protection | Record, continuation and reach-or-hold modelling | That a reconstructed record *is* the same continuing individual |
| Real-world deployment | Candidate engineering and commissioning obligations | A built and independently validated universal protection installation |

For detailed statements, dependencies and source-level evidence, use the **exact cited archive**, not a slogan or social summary.

## A short summary suitable for linking

> Universal Protection Theory (UPT/Snuffy) is an open research programme proposing formal priority rules for protecting existing lives, histories and freedoms. Its 29 October 2026 compilation reports extensive Lean 4 formalization and conditional control constructions. Independent reproduction, full physical enforcement and continuity of particular minds are **not** established. The author invites concrete countermodels and executable falsification: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23760830
