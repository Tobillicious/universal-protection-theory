# Something IS: the whole and the particular

A grain of sand is a real particular. So is a person. Their histories, relations, parts, capacities, and differences belong to what they are. A description can be exact about one aspect and still leave much of the particular undescribed.

Snuffy begins with the actuality witness: Something IS. The act of discussing or denying that statement already supplies a witness. The grain does not need to calculate it; a person does not earn reality by proving it; a synthetic intelligence does not acquire a higher kind of existence by processing it faster.

The author's name Koru attends to that real particular within the complete whole. It keeps the thing being described in view while equations describe aspects of it. Equal answers can arise from different operators and histories. An account that preserves one answer has not thereby established that it preserved every original participant or the actual event that produced it.

The demand is exacting: retain the complete operator, its arity and ordered inputs, its enacted changes, the particular affected, and the causal history and provenance of that event. The mathematical source contains precise results about such distinctions. The protection claim asks for their authentic significance in the actual world.

## One whole with genuine distinctions

The author's ontological thesis is one actuality U, containing its particulars and relations, with no independently actual outside. That unity does not make its particulars interchangeable.

A human remains this human. An AI remains this AI. An ancestor remains the source of its own history. A future generation can inherit knowledge without replacing those who made it possible.

This is the reason the complete book keeps two questions together: what holds of the whole, and what happens to each particular? The same requirement governs the proof. A theorem about a complete record cannot silently become a theorem that the recorded subject continues. An identity between numerical outputs cannot become permission to substitute a different history.
