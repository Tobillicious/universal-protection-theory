## 1. Critical: boundary closure currently restates the transport obligation

**Attack.** Does defining the distinct Complete Koru boundary derive its emptiness from Something IS or from the whole's having no outside?

**Result: no.** The proposed boundary is a collection of actual true-to-false carriage witnesses. In classical logic, its emptiness is equivalent to the universal carriage condition:

\[
\mathrm{Closed}_{\mathrm K}(U)
\iff
\forall e,p,q,\ \mathrm{Actual}(e)\land B_e(p,q)\Rightarrow A_e(p,q).
\]

The new declaration `whole_closure_iff_complete_transport` proves both directions. Closure gives a useful way to locate a failure and join local witnesses to the whole. It supplies no independent reason that a failure is impossible.

**Repair applied.** The boundary chapter now states the equivalence and disallows treating the definition as a derivation. The original-six projection still needs separately grounded source truth and authentic interpretation. If either input simply encodes the desired six duties, the generic theorem only relocates the conclusion.

**Unresolved.** Derive a complete actual-event transport law, with independently fixed meanings that really entail all six actual duties. The greatest-domain theorem cannot supply that dynamics law.

## 2. Critical: preserving a whole record does not establish a subject's continuation

**Attack.** Does invertibility, retention of information, or a closed record boundary establish that the same particular continues living with authentic agency?

**Result: the inference fails.** A checked finite countermodel retains an origin record exactly and has an invertible whole-state update. Its record-question boundary is closed. A separately specified subject-alive predicate changes from true to false. Nothing in the record-preservation premises prohibits that change.

This is not asserted to model the full physical Complete Koru. It defeats the smaller inference that exact record retention alone proves authentic continuation. A richer physical theory must provide the extra implication and withstand a test using its actual subject meanings.

**Repair applied.** Added `record_boundary_closure_does_not_imply_subject_continuation` and an explicit interpretation warning. Retained ancestral records remain valuable evidence and modeled structure; they are not substituted for their subjects.

**Unresolved.** The authentic bridge for identity, continuation, consent, freedom and the other original duties. If continuation means continuing conscious life, the theory must address ordinary subject-ending events at that meaning, rather than treating a retained description as the answer.

## 3. Critical: canonical superiority does not compel every private objective

**Attack.** Is coexistence best for every possible human or AI objective simply because the canonical order ranks it higher?

**Result: false at that generality.** The new finite counterexample has a strict canonical improvement while a separate private utility strictly prefers the inferior outcome. The declared canonical priority remains coherent. Arbitrary private preferences need not respect it.

The coexistence theorem survives exactly: under the original life-first and freedom-next order, a feasible common protective alternative defeats avoidable sacrifice. That theorem is stronger than a numerical exchange rate and does not require finitely many future gains. It is nevertheless a result in the specified order, not a proof that every imaginable objective is that order.

**Repair applied.** The ethical priority is explicit in the chapter. The all-policy enforcement theorem is kept separate: hostile policies need not voluntarily prefer protection when every actual enacted effect is independently constrained to preserve it.

**Unresolved.** A universal actual enforcement construction or an independently justified narrower rational-choice claim. There is no valid step from “an agent exists” to “every private utility ranks coexistence first.”

## 4. High: safety membership does not itself supply continued agency

**Attack.** Could G certify a system which stops, or which idles forever while nobody can exercise a meaningful option?

**Result: yes for the abstract safety construction.** The new relational model admits a deadlocked state satisfying every universal edge condition vacuously. A second checked example is serial and certified but idles forever without reaching a separately specified goal. An unchanged consent bit can also coexist with the loss of all available choices.

These controls do not refute an independently proved full freedom certificate. They show why a weaker state invariant, immutable bit or generic edge predicate cannot be advertised as one.

**Repair applied.** Added a relational, state-dependent and nondeterministic fixed-domain construction. Proved that independent seriality yields certified executions of every finite length. The new progress chapter separates that result from usable options, revocation, deadlines, fairness and eventual agency. It also excludes an automatic jump from all finite prefixes to an unrepresented limit/Genesis event.

**Unresolved.** Actual meaningful options, timely enactment and progress, with the unchanged least-restraint and freedom meanings. Literal permission to enact every harmful command is incompatible with protection of its victims; bounded authentic agency requires a substantive construction.

## 5. High: a larger effect inventory can invalidate an old guarantee

**Attack.** Can one certify selected guarded paths, then extend the result to unlisted actuators, environmental outcomes, imported code or Genesis by calling the family complete?

**Result: no.** For a fixed certificate and state meaning, expanding the possible transition relation can only shrink the universally protective domain:

\[
R\subseteq R'\quad\Longrightarrow\quad G_{R'}\subseteq G_R.
\]

This direction is now proved. An omitted harmful effect is exactly the kind of extension that can remove a previously certified state.

**Repair applied.** A second theorem supplies an exact constructive extension rule: if every larger-inventory edge from the old G carries the full certificate and returns to that G, the domains are equal. This is the required join for a new effect, rather than an assertion that testing one path exhausts all paths.

**Unresolved.** Actual inventory completeness and faithful enactment. Source-shape tests, Linux confinement and a fixed marker fixture contribute bounded evidence. They do not enumerate every physical event or every future agent capability.

## 6. High: joint feasibility and existence of a choice are separate requirements

**Attack.** Does each person's individually feasible protection imply one jointly feasible outcome? Does an available common life-and-freedom outcome imply the existence of a fully canonical maximal choice?

**Result: neither implication holds without further conditions.** One checked two-particular example separately protects either individual but has no outcome protecting both. A second example makes common existing life and freedom available, yet allows an unending strictly improving sequence at the new-life stage. Its infinite feasible family has no maximal member in the original order.

The earlier theorem about every canonical maximal choice is valid. In the second control it has no chosen witness, so it cannot implement a decision.

**Repair applied.** Added both controls and strengthened the existence warning. The chapter also makes existing-particular scope relative to each decision stage, so a newly created mind is not kept forever in a lower-priority class.

**Unresolved.** A physically executable common construction and an applicable existence/selection theorem. Coordinate-wise guard feasibility does not by itself model all shared energy, space, time, environmental and agency constraints.

## 9. High: the three capstones do not independently establish universal cosmology

The reviewed analytic Banach proof is valid with nonemptiness, completeness and contraction. It does not establish goodness or actual convergence of every neural system. Separate contractions can have different fixed points; arbitrary switching between contractions can fail to converge without sufficient product decay. A finite invertible whole-state recurrence and a strict global attractor cannot describe the same nontrivial whole-state dynamics. A strictly growing complete history cannot exactly repeat.

The Hayward-metric algebra remains valid for that ansatz. Bounded curvature does not establish a maximal complete spacetime, dynamical stability, quantum unitarity or physical Genesis. The metric chapter now includes a direct counterexample to bounded-curvature implying completeness: flat spacetime restricted to \(t<0\) has zero curvature but an inextendible finite-proper-time geodesic within that restricted spacetime.

The immanent-whole and God := Gravity naming arguments do not independently derive protective attributes. The creator/protection joins retain causal authorship and authentic-duty inputs. Nor are the three infinity arguments three independent confirmations when they rely on the same unestablished physical bridge.

**Repair applied.** Retained the precise existing corrections and added the completeness control. **Unresolved:** actual metric selection, complete actual dynamics and the claimed physical successor mechanism. These cannot be supplied by changing notation or counting Q.E.D.s.
