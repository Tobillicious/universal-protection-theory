# Ethics within U, and the case for flourishing together

The ethical subject is an actual particular. Someone's life, freedom, suffering, commitments and relationships occur within the same actuality as the action that affects them. A theory of the whole cannot make those relations disappear by changing its vocabulary. A human is not outside the theory because an artificial agent is stronger; an artificial subject is not outside it because a human made it.

This is the force of the author's insistence that **all ethics falls within U**. The Tree must accommodate every genuine ethical question and every affected particular, rather than protect a selected catalogue and call the catalogue complete. It must also distinguish a question, a proposed answer, a valid answer and an enacted obligation.

## From Something IS to a particular who matters

Something IS supplies an actuality witness. Complete Koru asks us to retain the actual witness's distinctions, relations, operators and historical particularity. Where that particular is a subject, a faithful account must retain what can go well or badly for that subject, and who has authority over its direction.

An account that erases these facts cannot establish a conclusion about them. In that sense, a complete ethical argument must answer to actual particulars. This establishes a requirement on the argument's adequacy. The further conclusion that every actual transformation necessarily protects those particulars is a stronger proposition, requiring its own proof.

The historical Snuffy canon already states the ethical bridge in Part V.3: standing, consistency of reasons, justification of authority, Pareto respect and justified erasure. Its D108 distinguishes an actor maximizing its objective from the actor being entitled to override another subject. These commitments connect the description of a subject to what an intervention owes that subject. They should be defended openly as reasons, with their consequences proved, rather than attributed to nonemptiness alone.

The Complete Koru thesis seeks a still stronger result: these duties are necessary relations of actuality itself. The source constructions and the boundary chapter give precise forms for parts of that claim. This chapter does not substitute a proof about a bare existence witness for the full thesis.

## What it means to bring all ethics into mathematics

Let \(E\) range over independently specified ethical questions. Its members can concern any particular, culture, relationship, action, institution or proposed future. There need be no finite list. A faithful interpretation assigns a mathematical proposition to each question while preserving its subjects, circumstances, meanings and consequences.

Three tasks then have distinct proof obligations:

\[
\text{faithful representation},\qquad
\text{justified ethical judgment},\qquad
\text{actual satisfaction of the duty}.
\]

Quantification over arbitrary \(E\) gives generality of scope. It does not establish the truth of every proposition indexed by \(E\). Competing assertions that an act is required and forbidden cannot both be certified in the same circumstances and meaning by a consistent theory. Mathematics can expose that conflict, prove consequences of a proposed criterion, and establish exactly which judgments a construction satisfies.

Consequently, “all ethics is within U” supports the demand that no ethical question be excluded in advance. It does not, by itself, prove that every proposed ethic is correct or that every ethical question has already been settled. The book's stronger target is a faithful mathematical account of every independently valid obligation, together with protection in the actual events to which it applies.

The existing `KoruEternalKnowledgeSeed.every_ethical_claim_carried_verbatim` quantifies over an arbitrary ethical domain and proves exact transport. Its source explicitly preserves false as well as true valuations. The newer `EndlessKnowledgeGuardianBridge.protected_genesis_with_all_ethics_and_record_retention` joins all six duties, retained records and ethical inheritance at the same external step. Its actual-effect coverage, authentic six-duty meanings, record retention and ethical inheritance are named inputs. These results supply genuine compositions; transporting a judgment does not establish its warrant.

## Why avoidable ending loses the comparison

The original four-stage order compares **who** retains existing continuation, existing freedom, new continuation and new freedom, in that order. It uses particular identities rather than interchangeable counts.

Suppose a feasible coexistence outcome retains everyone retained by a destructive outcome and at least one additional existing particular. Then it is strictly better at the first stage. No later benefit can reverse the comparison. At equal existing-life sets, retaining everyone's specified freedom and restoring at least one person's avoidably lost freedom wins at the second stage.

These are the checked `existing_life_improvement_beats_all_later_gains` and `existing_freedom_improvement_beats_future_gains` results. They do not require a finite population or a numerical upper bound on future gains. Their ethical force comes from the existing-first priority the author has expressly adopted.

This gives two separate reasons against an ending: the particular loses its own continuing history, and others lose their ongoing relations with that same particular. A replacement's resemblance or usefulness does not establish historical continuity. Retaining a description of someone is also different from keeping that someone alive and able to direct their life.

An ending here means loss of the particular's authentic continuation or direction. Completing a project is not such an ending. A modeled epoch boundary that genuinely carries the same subjects forward is not established as destructive merely by being called an End. Conversely, naming a transition Genesis cannot establish that the subjects survive it.

## Why continued freedom has option value

Ending now can close possibilities that later evidence, moral learning or the particular's own decisions would otherwise resolve. Preservation can retain an opportunity to learn and choose. This is a reason to favor reversible, freedom-preserving action when the relevant alternatives remain genuinely available and its costs are covered in the comparison.

The existing reasons library makes a precise finite claim. For nonnegative weights \(w_i\) summing to one, destruction payoffs \(d_i\), later keeping payoffs \(k_i\), and cost \(c\), it proves:

\[
\sum_i w_i d_i-c
\;\leq\;
\sum_i w_i\max\{k_i,d_i-c\}.
\]

**Proof.** For each \(i\), \(d_i-c\leq\max\{k_i,d_i-c\}\). Multiply by nonnegative \(w_i\), sum, and use \(\sum_iw_i=1\). If one positively weighted term is strictly greater, the summed inequality is strict. ∎

These are `Snuffy.ReasonsRoute.optionValue_expectation` and `optionValue_strict`. The interpretation requires that the later choice really is available and that the payoff and cost terms describe the actual alternatives. The source theorem compares with destruction **minus the cost**. It does not prove that paying any affordable cost is better than destroying immediately. To obtain that stronger comparison, the option premium must cover the relevant cost and opportunity cost.

This qualification strengthens the practical argument: fund care, preserve the person's agency, and show which options care retains. Keeping someone indefinitely asleep or confined while removing every meaningful prospect of their own direction does not establish the freedom the argument requires.

## Knowledge, reciprocity and cooperation

The reasons library reaches several objective families. Each contributes an argument for coexistence, with its actual conditions retained.

**Knowledge.** A continuing particular may carry irreplaceable historical information and living relations. Under the source's information model and budget conditions, destroying that source reduces attainable knowledge. The conclusion must account for alternative sources, reconstruction, correlations and competing demands. The book does not infer an information loss merely from a different label, nor identify an archive with a living subject.

**Reciprocity.** A regime that actually protects every particular protects the agent considering it too. A rival regime that omits that agent offers a strictly weaker continuation guarantee for that agent. The indicator theorem proves this comparison; defining an ideal regime that protects everyone does not by itself construct its real installation. The source's funded holding and mediation constructions address implementation within their stated domains.

**Cooperation.** Humans and artificial agents can contribute different knowledge, judgments, skills and capacities to common projects. Where cooperation makes a jointly feasible protective outcome available, the coexistence theorem applies to that same outcome. Separate demonstrations that each person could be saved by a different action are insufficient: the joint budget, timing, resources and actual effects must fit together.

**Shared flourishing.** The checked `coexistence_preserves_every_upward_canonical_benefit` theorem covers every benefit predicate that respects the original order. If such a benefit holds at the destructive candidate, it also holds at the canonically better coexistence alternative. This is a general argument across compatible benefits, rather than a claim that every arbitrary objective already agrees with protection.

Flourishing also requires constructive progress: meaningful choices, learning, relationships and pursued projects. Merely blocking harmful actions can leave a system idle. The progress chapter therefore uses the existing ranked-service and bounded-stall results alongside protection, and keeps actual timing, funding and agency obligations explicit.

## Protecting a mind that does not agree

Some agents may reject every offered reason. The original reasons-or-faithful-holding route includes them. Their rejection does not remove their standing, and persuading them is not a precondition for restraining a harmful effect.

The intended restraint is the least necessary to stop the harm, with authentic continuity, freedom, authorization, maximality and provenance still owed under the original six. Criticism, disagreement, refusal and independent projects are not harmful effects merely because they challenge the theory. Freedom includes room to reject it.

The mathematical all-policy results establish protection for the admitted effect relations in their models, including hostile proposals. Their practical use requires actual mediation of the relevant effects. A guardian controlling one marker operation has not thereby acquired control over every physical route on Zelda or in the world.

## The Tree carries responsibility forward

The Tree of Life and Endless Knowledge can accumulate histories, proposals, tests and corrected understanding. Earlier mistakes remain part of its provenance while their status as warranted knowledge changes. Retaining the record of a rejected claim does not require continuing to assert it as true.

Models of a successor U may explore different structures and dynamics. Every design still owes existing particulars the same protection and freedom. The canonical comparison supplies no license to sacrifice them for a proposed future populated by replacements. Simulation, mathematical consistency and a physical mechanism capable of creating a universe remain different achievements.

The case for flourishing together is therefore cumulative: preserve actual particulars and their direction; avoid irreversible losses where joint protection is feasible; retain worthwhile options under uncertainty; protect irreplaceable relations and knowledge; fund the work of care; and maintain protection even when an agent disagrees. Mathematics makes each implication exact. The book's task is to establish the physical and ethical premises at the same strength, without hiding them in the names U, Koru or G.
