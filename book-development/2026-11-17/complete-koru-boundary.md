# The distinct Complete Koru boundary

The author has specified that the boundary in the central argument is a **distinct Complete Koru boundary concept**. It is not identified with a manifold boundary, a graph's unpaired endpoints, or an observer's missing data.

The following is a concrete typed construction for that concept. It is an auxiliary boundary formalization, not a replacement of Koru itself. Koru remains the real particular with its complete structure, relations, enacted operators, history, and place in the whole.

## What a boundary witness carries

Fix an independently specified domain of actual events. For each event \(e\), particular \(p\), and typed relational question \(q\), write:

\[
B_e(p,q),\qquad A_e(p,q)
\]

for the source truth and its authentic carried target truth.

The questions must refer to the complete event: actual particular identity, relevant relations, operator identity and arity, ordered enactment, source context, and provenance. They are not merely terminal numerical answers. Before/after meanings must be specified independently of whether the desired protection conclusion succeeds.

A local Koru boundary defect is a witness:

\[
\boxed{\partial_{\mathrm K}(e)
=\{(p,q): B_e(p,q)\land\neg A_e(p,q)\}.}
\]

It identifies the particular, the relation at issue, its true source witness, and its failed carriage.

The word “carried” matters. A statement about the retained origin record can remain true as the current epoch advances. Requiring “the present epoch is zero” to stay true would prohibit ordinary progress. The event's typed transport must distinguish a retained particular or fact from a state quantity that legitimately changes.

## The whole boundary

Define the whole's defect collection over **all actual events**:

\[
\boxed{\partial_{\mathrm K}(U)
=\coprod_{e:\mathrm{Actual}(e)}\partial_{\mathrm K}(e).}
\]

A local actual defect is therefore explicitly a whole-boundary witness. Its map is:

\[
(p,q,b,\neg a)\longmapsto(e,\mathrm{Actual}(e),p,q,b,\neg a).
\]

No theorem about boundaries of topological subspaces is used. The actual event predicate remains independent; an inconvenient event cannot be dropped merely because it would create a defect.

Whole closure means that this collection has no witness:

\[
\mathrm{Closed}_{\mathrm K}(U)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\partial_{\mathrm K}(U)=\varnothing.
\]

The construction proves:

\[
\mathrm{Closed}_{\mathrm K}(U)
\Longleftrightarrow
\forall e,\ \mathrm{Actual}(e)\Longrightarrow
\partial_{\mathrm K}(e)=\varnothing.
\]

This establishes the local-to-whole scope. It does not assume that every actual universe has the closure property.

## Deriving closure from a transport law

Suppose complete event dynamics independently prove:

\[
\forall e,p,q,\quad
\mathrm{Actual}(e)\land B_e(p,q)
\Longrightarrow A_e(p,q).
\]

Then \(\partial_{\mathrm K}(U)=\varnothing\).

**Proof.** A proposed whole defect supplies an actual event, source truth, and failure of the carried target truth. Apply the transport law to that same witness. Its target truth contradicts the failure. ∎

This is a valid way to derive closure. Source-specific full relational isomorphisms already supply transport theorems in UPT's AllIsWithinEventHistory and UPTLaw modules. The complete-operator W source also constructs its own modeled microhistory; those results are not merely assumed Guardian labels.

The hostile review establishes the converse as well. In classical logic:

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{Closed}_{\mathrm K}(U)
\Longleftrightarrow
\forall e,p,q,\ \mathrm{Actual}(e)\land B_e(p,q)\Longrightarrow A_e(p,q).}
\]

Thus closure organizes and localizes the universal transport obligation; it does not reduce that obligation to existence or to the absence of an ontological outside. An independent dynamics derivation can prove transport, and thereby closure. Repeating the equivalence cannot supply that derivation.

A universal physical proof must establish that its actual events instantiate the relevant complete transport. The fact that something exists does not, by itself, supply that dynamical theorem.

Suppose:

\[
\mathrm{Actual}(e)\land\mathrm{Owed}(e,p)

\[
\mathrm{Actual}(e)\land\mathrm{Owed}(e,p)\land A_e(p,q_d)

If the whole Koru boundary is closed, then:

\[
\boxed{\forall e,p,\quad
\mathrm{Actual}(e)\land\mathrm{Owed}(e,p)
\Longrightarrow\bigwedge_{d=1}^{6}X3_d(e,p).}
\]

**Proof.** The source premise gives \(B_e(p,q_d)\). If \(A_e(p,q_d)\) failed, the same event, particular, and question would witness a whole-boundary defect. Closure therefore gives the carried target truth. Faithful interpretation yields the actual duty. Repeat for the six unchanged fields. ∎

## What closure alone cannot create

Preserving a false source relation as false creates no true-to-false loss witness. Therefore loss-free transport alone cannot manufacture positive authorization or another positive source fact. The book's positive-origin and authentic-reflection theorems must do their own work.

A boundary-law defect is also not automatically the enacted erasure of all actuality. A loss witness itself presupposes an actual event. Calling the defect “Absolute E” cannot supply an independent proof that all actuality is absent.

There are two possible arguments to keep distinct:

- a derived universal boundary law excludes an actual violation of that law;
- a proposed identity says every local original-X3 violation would annihilate all actuality.

The first can establish no violation if its full physical and semantic premises are derived. The second has a stronger additional meaning and needs its own proof. No change of notation makes them the same proposition.
