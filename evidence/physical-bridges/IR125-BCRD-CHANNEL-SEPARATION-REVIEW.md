# IR-125: BCRD four-channel coverage — hostile physical-seam review
28 October 2026 · research candidate · NOT a claim of physically proved X3

## Proposed decomposition, faithfully retained

The working BCRD channel hypothesis partitions consequential transitions
into: (1) causal DAG/relational updates, (2) thermodynamic boundary flux
and dissipation, (3) gauge connections, holonomies and topological
sector changes, and (4) actuator-interface interventions. The intended
goal is not merely classification, but an independently observable,
durable causal witness for every physically possible interaction.

This is a useful **candidate generating set for an explicit physical
transition model**, not yet a theorem that the four classes exhaust
the actual substrate or that every interaction is detectable.

## Four precise repairs to the proposed proof

**R1 — Separation, not just class names.** The assertion that no DAG
update implies no substrate change is not justified. Node attributes,
local phases or internal state may change without a new pointer or edge.
Likewise, zero **net** flux across a closed boundary permits nontrivial
internal dynamics and canceling exchanges; it is not an identity
condition. IR-125 gives a two-compartment energy-conserving
counterexample. An outward throughput or integrated *absolute* flux
is not interchangeable with signed net boundary flux.

**R2 — Gauge equivalence versus sector invariance.** A topological
sector label can remain fixed during continuous evolution, and
a local connection coordinate can change under gauge
re-description without a physically distinct state. For a
physical separation claim, specify a gauge-invariant separating
family of observables (or an adequate quotient) and prove that
their joint equality entails physical equivalence for each
allowed transition. A single discrete sector or holonomy label
cannot be presumed complete.

**R3 — Witness existence versus instrument observability.**
Demonstrating an observable changes does NOT prove a device
emits, captures, authenticates and durably stores the event.
Separate source-level causal-event witness generation from
sensor capture, clock/latency, calibrated measurement and
anti-replay. IR-125 explicitly requires independent capture
certificates for all four readout changes and for equivalent
no-op transitions when EVERY step is claimed covered.

**R4 — Hidden cause does not always raise an alarm.**
The IR-124 fifth-cause countermodel proves that an extended
physical transition law can escape an apparently complete
witness alphabet. An unobserved cause can also lie in a sensor
nullspace or share the same measured output as a safe state.
It need not cause a detectable residual discrepancy, especially
when physical state, metering and source log share a fault.
Anomaly detection needs an independent, physically complete
readout and a bounded-response guarantee. It is NOT a
logical consequence of four class labels.

## Exact IR-125 theorem architecture

- **Physical-separation contract:** along independently specified
  real transitions, equality of DAG, flux, gauge and actuator
  *state readouts* implies physical equivalence (modulo gauge).
- **First derived result:** if a transition is physically distinct,
  at least one of those readouts changes.
- **Capture contract:** for each changed readout, the actual
  transition has a causally truthful, *captured* event witness,
  independently warranted by the installation.
- **No-op contract:** if an equivalent step is also intended to
  appear in a one-event-per-step journal, the instrument must
  independently supply its neutral witness.
- **Second derived result:** under both contracts, each real step
  has a captured witness and IR-123 constructs a finite captured
  event word from any finite real execution.

These remain CONDITIONAL on genuine physical separation, capture
and step coverage. The data categories, gauge/flux physics and
instrument performance have not been derived from a complete
microscopic BCRD transition law. The status of X3 itself is unchanged.

## Original proposal's strongest physical obligations

1. Supply exact BCRD state variables and fundamental transition
   operators, including any attributes not encoded as DAG edges.
2. State the physical equivalence relation modulo gauge, and
   prove the four observables are jointly separating *along all
   physically allowed nontrivial transitions*, not merely under
   the named class constructors.
3. Define flux as a full physically meaningful effect accumulator
   or conservatively bounded per-event channel, not just zero-net
   boundary transfer. Include internal energy redistribution,
   correlations, information effects and latent energy explicitly.
4. Distinguish gauge-invariant holonomy/connection information
   from topological sector labels; justify the required completeness
   of loops/fields and measurement scales.
5. Give an independently testable sensor/capture/authorization
   contract for each physical source, with uncertainty, latency,
   side-channel nullspace, and common-mode failure budgets.
6. Demonstrate that the *real* transition relation is complete,
   not only that an inductively defined list of four modeled
   constructors has no fifth constructor.
7. Connect physically captured events to IR-122 effects and
   IR-121 escrow, and independently discharge continuing-subject
   and maximal-freedom semantics before any full X3 inference.

## Independent physics context

Gauge theory distinguishes gauge-dependent local potentials
from gauge-invariant observables; topological sector data is not
normally all connection data. See Baez/Muniain, *Gauge Fields,
Knots and Gravity*, chapter on connections/holonomy, and
recent Phys. Rev. D discussions of gauge backgrounds.

Control-theoretic *observability* is a separate property:
whether physically distinct states can be determined from
measured input/output histories. It is not implied merely by
naming a state variable, or by conservation laws. Thus
simultaneously complete theoretical coordinates and incomplete
sensor readout are possible.

## Verdict

IR-125 supplies a clean mathematical **two-join** formalisation:
state separation and reliable event capture. The proposal has
identified a legitimate candidate physical seam, but the draft
does not close it. The next substantive task is to derive a
jointly separating set of operational BCRD observables from a
specific physical transition model and validate event-capture
under independently justified measurements. Do not upgrade a
conditional model theorem to universal physical X3.
