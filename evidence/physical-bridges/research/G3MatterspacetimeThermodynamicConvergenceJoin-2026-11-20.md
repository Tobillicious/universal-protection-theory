## Physical-law discipline

determines an entire microhistory. Neither quantum unitarity nor source
injectivity alone establishes universal no-harm. In general relativity,
local stress-energy covariant conservation does not assert a single
globally conserved energy for arbitrary curved spacetime.
The second law's coarse-grained entropy increase is compatible with
information-retaining microdynamics; it is not by itself an ethical
six-duty law. Physical convergence also needs a specified topology,
dynamics and a demonstrable contraction/attraction property.
