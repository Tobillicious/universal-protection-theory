# IR-67 — Infinite outcomes with multiple original lexicographic priorities

IR-66 establishes a full-feasible maximal outcome for finite-obstruction
retention families under the original `UPTExist.Aligned` inclusion order
and a one-priority `LexAlignedL` restriction.

IR-67 directly uses the untouched `UPTExist.LexBetter` comparator
at multiple priorities. Both proofs operate over **infinite full
feasible outcome types**, without a finite profile quotient, finite
candidate catalogue or narrowed safe-menu selection.

## Positive result 1: arbitrarily many finite original lex stages

An outcome is a family of retention sets indexed by priority
(`Nat → Nat → Prop`). The physical-like feasibility predicate is
explicitly **separable between stages**: each stage's full retention
set must satisfy that stage's own finite-obstruction predicate.
The unrestricted outcome space may be uncountable.

IR-66 supplies a globally inclusion-maximal full-feasible retention
set for each stage. Induction on an **arbitrary finite list of priority
indices** shows that no full-feasible outcome can strictly improve
the selected tuple in the original `UPTExist.LexBetter` order.
This includes one, two, or any finite number of priority stages; no
physical computational chooser or feasibility oracle is claimed.

The theorem does NOT apply when the real original feasible set couples
what may be retained at different priority stages.

## Positive result 2: coupled two-stage infinite model

In an independent, distinct synthetic model, the first-stage retention
of particular 0 conflicts with second-stage retention of particular 0.
This is an actual **cross-stage feasibility constraint** on a pair of
infinite retention sets.

The lexically chosen full-feasible outcome retains all first-stage
facts and all second-stage facts except the conflicting 0. A direct
source-level proof establishes `UPTExist.LexAlignedL` against
**every outcome in the original coupled feasible family**: stage-one
strict improvement is impossible; any stage-two strict improvement
must add the forbidden member.

This demonstrates the source comparator can be used correctly in
at least one nonseparable infinite feasibility world, but does NOT
prove a generic coupled-stage existence theorem.

## Hostile control

With zero independent forbidden patterns at both priority stages,
each independent IR-66 optimum retains all particulars. The product
of those optima VIOLATES the synthetic cross-stage constraint.
Therefore substituting 'independently maximal at each stage' for
'jointly feasible original lexicographic maximality' is invalid.

## Remaining independent obligations

- Prove the original physically feasible X3 outcome universe has
  finitary/local witness properties, a valid directed-limit
  completion or another true infinite-order maximality principle;
- Derive generic multi-stage lex maximality under genuine coupled
  feasibility, not only separable worlds or one chosen toy example;
- Show the original full freedom and historical particular
  semantics are faithfully represented by stagewise retention sets;
- Prove physical realization of the chosen jointly feasible outcome
  and impartial/consent-respecting conflict resolution;
- Demonstrate real service, continuity, physical route coverage,
  effect envelopes and actual device enforcement.

**No X3 weakening, no archive rewrite, no universal physical claim.**
IR-67 extends the independent formal logic only.
