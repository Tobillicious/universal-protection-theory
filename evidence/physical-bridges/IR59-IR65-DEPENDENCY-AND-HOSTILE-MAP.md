## Critical distinctions no code should silently collapse

1. **Nine constructors vs archive nine transitions.** IR-60 chose
   independent synthetic cases. No bijection or source-level equivalence
   to the Zenodo 23599528 original transitions has been established.
2. **Modeled side-channel completeness vs physical causal inventory.**
   Enumerating a typed `bypass` does not detect, prevent or bound
   real unrepresented bypass routes.
3. **Finite conditional trace vs the future.** Each theorem quantifies
   over finite certified traces. No unbounded-time safety guarantee
   follows without a valid induction/coinduction interface and persistent
   physical assumptions; per-event certification may fail in the future.
4. **Prospective check vs physically enforced gate.** Lean's `if`
   branches guarantee the simulated transition, not sensor freshness,
   unforgeable authorization, atomic final hardware application or
   actuator control of uncontrolled exogenous channels.
5. **Physical risk scalar vs real protection.** The model uses a
   monotone Nat register; real harms may have multiple dimensions,
   nonlinear coupling, negative/reversible increments or transient
   spikes. IR-51–54 remain necessary for continuous physical paths.
6. **Residual conservation vs actual debit.** Software escrow totals
   cannot substitute for physical causal measurement or exclude an
   uncharged event.
7. **Aggregate/pointwise positive service vs the original freedom
   bundle.** A positive observed-service counter need not establish
   a particular person's autonomy, rights, continuity, consent,
   irreversible non-ending, or the fullest jointly feasible freedoms.
8. **Uniform per-agent vs total fleet.** IR-57 uses bounded channel
   degree/impact to derive *local* margin, not an unlimited total
   resource cap. IR-65 requires every agent's complete interface.
9. **Infinite feasibility.** IR-56 rules out an everywhere-faithful
   finite retention-profile quotient for the original chainKey family.
   None of IR-59–65 settles full-feasible maximality or X3.
10. **Tragic choices and impartiality.** Shared order need not produce
    a unique maximal outcome; a uniform lottery over maximal outcomes
    can give individuals unequal chances. Implemented choice and
    per-individual fairness remain separate.
11. **Nonempty physical population.** `∀ a : Agent` is vacuous if
    the type is empty; there must be physically attested protected
    particulars and a sound account of which actual histories are
    represented.

## The highest-value next experiments / proofs

- **P1 (physical):** pick one actually available non-destructive
  instrument with externally inspectable actions, sensors, control
  paths and delayed effects. Produce a causal route census,
  challenge it with independent fault injection, and collect signed
  traces of physical outcomes.
- **P2 (source + physical):** define explicit real-to-model transition
  and timestamp refinement, including unobserved motion and
  actuator cancellation. Fail closed on missing events.
- **P3 (physical):** independently measure effect-error envelopes and
  residual bank debits, verify the lifetime of any claimed upper
  bound, and test revoked vs fresh credentials after power loss.
- **P4 (service):** demonstrate a physically observed, authenticated
  recipient-specific service effect, not an internal counter or
  journal entry. Compare real readings against IR-64 lower
  correspondence and test no-release starvation.
- **P5 (mathematical):** derive full-feasible maximality over the
  original potentially infinite outcomes without an unsupported
  finite profile quotient; then connect impartial selection to
  the actually implemented policy.
- **P6 (semantic):** explicitly define protected particular,
  history continuation and the full freedom/independence bundle.
  Test replacement/ending and tragic conflict countermodels.
