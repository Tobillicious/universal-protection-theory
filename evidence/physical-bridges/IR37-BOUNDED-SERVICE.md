# IR-37 — Bounded service is a separate obligation from safety

A system can maintain a physical safety invariant by holding forever.
That is not enough to show that the originally promised service or
freedom is delivered to anyone.

This theorem establishes a conditional *universal bounded-progress*
result. From a protected state with rank N, any length-N modeled
disturbance history reaches the service goal if:
- every physical/disturbance step preserves the protected invariant;
- delivery of service persists through subsequent modeled steps;
- before service, **every** possible next step strictly decreases a
  natural-number rank.

The theorem uses nonempty disturbance space. The rank and the final
service condition must be defined for an actual physical instrument.

Negative controls include a forever-holding safe controller and a
process in which some disturbances help while others stall. The latter
cannot establish an unconditional deadline.

The result does not demonstrate the required decreasing rank for
any actual plant, original X3 outcome, or physical mind. It cannot
justify coercive use of the research or claims of imminent emergency.
