"prove actual SI choice cannot select a dominated feasible action"

choice can be intercepted; the realized *effect* is what the
physical preservation architecture certifies. The individual
remains owed protection.

installation has physically established each of the required
source/event/fault/cut correspondences; edition 4.4 itself
explicitly retained `G1` full installation/source and `G2`

requirements, **not demands for voluntary benevolence**. They
must be evaluated against all original pre-key source proofs,

The original sources distinguish that claim from event-
