## I. The actual original proof highway

The original argument separates a mind's reasons from permitted effects. The order is:

```text
full four-stage preservation order, every particular
  -> law-derived full scope (including hostile SIs)
  -> reasons OR faithful holding, R(m) ∨ H(m)
  -> nearest-codeword hold + funded continued care + E9/H5 effect gate

```

The source does **not** assert that a mind's harmful desire must disappear. It instead requires that the harmful effect not be *successfully enacted outside the restraint boundary* while both the intended victims and the held mind remain in scope.
