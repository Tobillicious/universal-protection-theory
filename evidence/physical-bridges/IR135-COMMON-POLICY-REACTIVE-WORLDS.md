## Mathematically proved

1. reactiveDisturbances: for a source and world function (State, Action) -> Disturbance, extract the successive actual adversarial disturbances generated AFTER actions are chosen. Do not substitute a prerecorded list.
2. reactive_trace_is_source_trace: the action-reactive history is exactly the ordinary source-executed history with its own induced disturbance list. This is an exact execution adapter, not an arbitrary realization predicate.
3. same_policy_worldwise_original_maximality_and_continuation: fix ONE policy, source, initial state and finite horizon. For EVERY admitted world in the stipulated family, prove (a) its selected trace is physically source-reachable, (b) it is maximal compared to ALL rival policies IN THE SAME world under the original FOUR priorities, and (c) every originally owed particular has declared continuation and all specified required executable options at EVERY state on that actual selected trace. The proof reuses IR134 stagewise faithful-profile audit and IR116 cumulative pathwise duties. It does not derive the existence of such a policy or physical faithfulness.
4. each_reactive_world_has_its_own_successful_policy: two explicitly defined Bool worlds, each individually permitting a policy that reaches the same desired result.
5. each_world_separate_success_does_not_yield_common_policy: those worlds have no ONE observation-only policy that attains the target in both. Therefore forall world exists policy does NOT entail exists policy forall world. There is no anticipation of which environment the controller faces.

## Why this remains conditional

- The profile-obstruction hObstructions premise requires original-order loss/equality witnesses for ALL profiles in EACH admitted world and the SAME preselected policy. It is close to a complete maximality certificate, not supplied by actual physics.
- hCoverage and hFaithful require every rival source-executable outcome to be represented without dropping any original priority-stage particular; finite faithful profiles can fail for infinite worlds (IR-56). IR-66–71 supply alternate conditional infinite-case routes, not automatically this theorem's assumptions.
- hMonotone and hRobust are independent source-level physical/semantic predicates, with the historical original-particular and freedom obligations left intact. They must not be mistaken for proved identity semantics.
- The world is State x Action -> Disturbance; the real complete state must include all attacker memory, delays, unobserved effects, outside entrants, remote action and relevant causation. This completeness is NOT established by naming an input 'world'.
- The horizon is finite, and option availability does not guarantee actual service, timely exercise or positive freedom. IR-43 and IR-134 hostile stutter countermodels remain live.
- There is no independent physical establishment of the source transition, actual all-mind founding/reach, common resource bounds, genuine authorization, objective identity, installation, indefinite reliability or the full original physically feasible outcome family.

This is a source-level same-policy conditional composition and a constructive counterexample to swapping quantifiers; no universal physical X3 proof.
