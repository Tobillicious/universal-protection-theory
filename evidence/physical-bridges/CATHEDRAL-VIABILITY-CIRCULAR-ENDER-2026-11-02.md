## The paradox correctly separated by its intended target

The author's existence-first premise is that physics presupposes mathematics. An internal operation cannot invalidate mathematics itself; an event in one complete fixed history cannot make its own historical occurrence logically false.

If an absolute ender is defined as a mathematical agent that abolishes mathematical truth, that objective contradicts its own description. But a different target—physically ending a continuing subject within an otherwise existing mathematical cosmos—is not automatically self-contradictory. Full UPT protects actual particulars' continuation and agency, not only consistency of historical propositions. The missing join must prove that real BCRD source laws exclude those physical endings, not change the definition of an ending.

The further hypothesis that time is circular warrants its own mathematical tests. A circular history is nonempty and has no final phase in its cyclic order, but circular time by itself does not make independently defined subject activity true at all phases.

## A. Reuse the existing IR-72 infinite-horizon result

Original [IR-72 source theorem](https://github.com/Tobillicious/universal-protection-theory/blob/main/evidence/physical-bridges/PhysicalBridges/IRInfiniteHistoryCompactness.lean) proves: with two available immediate actions, if a state is robustly viable at every finite horizon against all modeled disturbances, a single (possibly noncomputable) policy exists preserving safety at every natural-number time and for every disturbance stream.

New uncompiled Lean candidate CathedralViabilitySelection.lean imports that existing result, and under explicit source-to-physical state correspondence plus source-predicate-to-actual-physical-protection reflection, derives existence of a policy that keeps the physically interpreted predicate at every natural-number indexed state.

It does not claim the actual BCRD physical source satisfies those premises, nor replace original X3's physical continuation, maximal freedom and consent with a Boolean safety indicator.

## B. Existence does not entail actual physical selection

Consider a two-action mathematical source, with Keep preserving an active state and End changing it to inactive. Keep satisfies every finite viability horizon and yields an infinite safe policy by IR-72. Yet an available policy selecting End produces an inactive state.

Thus existence of a policy that protects at all times and against every modeled disturbance does NOT logically imply that all physically selected policies protect.

The missing source theorem is actual guard/selection mediation—not whether a good mathematical policy can be imagined.

This is an abstract logical countercontrol and not a fully physically established BCRD ender.

## C. Circular time: invariant structure versus subject continuation

Exact two-phase cyclic time T={0,1}, successor F(0)=1 and F(1)=0, gives a bijection with F²=id. Both phases exist in one complete mathematical cycle.

Let P(0)=true and P(1)=false, representing a separate toy active predicate. The cycle returns and its mathematical history remains true, but the predicate is not true at every phase. No full original-X3 physical continuity follows from periodicity alone.

Positive exact theorem: on a transitive cycle, if P holds at one witnessing phase AND the actual physically derived successor preserves P on every admitted phase transition, P holds at all phases. The second premise must come from the true BCRD source, not from declaring any nonprotective operation inadmissible by definition. PR #202 also addresses holonomy/identity correspondence across valid cuts.

## D. The accumulation or limit seam

Let x_n=1/n for each positive natural n, and P(x) be x>0. Every finite x_n satisfies P, but their limit x=0 does not. An actual physical limit stage is not another ordinary successor step. If physical BCRD includes such limit points, an independent limit-closure/source-semantics result is needed. A finite discrete cyclic history need not have any such limit stage.

This source observation is compatible with IR-72's stronger infinite natural-time viability result, which does not by itself assert a physical omega-limit state beyond all natural indices.
