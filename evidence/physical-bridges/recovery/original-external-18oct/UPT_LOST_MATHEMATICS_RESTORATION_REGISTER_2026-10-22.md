In later endowed-plant models, the **end** can be the binding date after assuming or demonstrating commissioning and adequate early physical capacity. ## 3. The one most important substantive loss

The greatest lost *join*, not merely a lost chapter, is **the missing external-agent reach-or-hold mechanism**. A assigns noncompliant agents an equilibrium/keeper-coverage channel outside any named care plant (conditionally on P3 and P4c). The jurisdictional formulation in B–E turns its attention to agents within a physical/cryptographic installation. F's Chapter 16 expands the **normative** domain to all particulars and describes faithful holds, but does not on its own prove a nonempty funded coalition that can restrain *every* external agent or protect all non-minded particulars. This leaves two distinct missing implications:

1. **From feasible caretaker plant to credible external-agent interception**: who acts, on what signal, under what authority and latency, with what resources, and what does 'held' guarantee in an external physical causal network?
2. **From protection of agents to preservation of ALL particulars**: how are non-minded histories and relations preserved in their own direction; when is an archival record a different object rather than the original history?

This is the correct place to restore the 18 Oct mathematics into the newer architecture, not by copying 18 Oct's headline into Paper 11.

### Candidate G1/G2 bridging proposition (UNPROVED as a complete physical application)

Fix one common transition system, a time horizon H, and a precisely specified domain `D = hosted ∪ externally mediated`. Suppose:

- **initialisation:** with failure probability ≤ ε_init, the protective structure is founded before the first relevant irreversible event;
- **hosted lane:** for all host-covered harm processes the relevant physical/identity invariant is preserved except event of probability ≤ ε_host;
- **external lane:** for every certified external class the reached-or-held controller prevents needless ending except event of probability ≤ ε_ext;
- **interface:** switching between lanes preserves the protected lineage/causal obligations except event of probability ≤ ε_join;
- **common-model guarantee:** the same resource, clock, adversarial strategy, authorization and fidelity definitions apply to all four clauses.

Then by the union bound on the events (no cross-lane independence assumed):

`P(all covered histories satisfy their specified invariant through H) ≥ 1 − (ε_init + ε_host + ε_ext + ε_join)`.

**Scope limitation:** this proves a bound only for genuinely **covered** histories under the listed conditions. It does not derive that all external agents are covered, that any such shared system exists, or that preserving a record is identical to preserving a history. These are G3/G5/G6/G7 obligations. The *domain of concern* can remain ALL without setting the uncovered remainder to zero.
