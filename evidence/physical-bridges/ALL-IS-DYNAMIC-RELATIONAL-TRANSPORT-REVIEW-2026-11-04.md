## Source-faithful foundation

An intelligence and everything it does occur *within* that actuality. The assertion that an "ender" could literally annihilate mathematics/actuality itself is distinct from an agent's ability to cause a **local first violation** of another particular's continuity, freedom, authorization or other original-X3 duties.
