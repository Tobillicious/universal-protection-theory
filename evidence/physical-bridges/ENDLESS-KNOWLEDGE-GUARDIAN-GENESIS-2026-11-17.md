## 4. Strengthening G2 without redefining freedom

Existing `Snuffy.G2Held.AuthorisedFree` says every effect outside `P.harmful` has an admitted H5 effect transition if the agent is active. This quantifies over an admissible *modelled* action set, NOT the physical presence of a meaningful safe option. If the complement of `P.harmful` is empty, the universal quantifier holds vacuously. Moreover, `held_source_top` assumes source drift/budget/solvency, active slots and a funded family.

For a nonvacuous, actual-world G2, seek existing proof terms or independently establish ALL of:

**G2-1 — Actual agency:** for every owed p at a given stage, at least one independently evidenced, meaningful protected continuation/opportunity exists. Prove the existence of an enabled action or justified protected HOLD, rather than using a vacuous forall.

**G2-2 — Authentic authorization:** each offered/enacted opportunity is actually within p's authentic authorization and coherent with *all six* original X3 obligations. A source bit or signature alone may be inadequate.

**G2-3 — Sufficient resources:** real physical resource/accounting transitions enforce H5-style reserve, nonnegative custody, no unfunded work and harmful-effect refusal. Formally: derive `physicalStep → H5.Step` for an independently defined physicalStep and legitimate resource projection. The mere law of energy conservation does NOT entail consent, a positive feasible option or no harm.

**G2-4 — Fidelity of feasible family:** `maximal` compares ACTUAL physically obtainable protected outcomes, not a preferred artificial set, while preserving everyone owed protection.

**G2-5 — Endlessly growing discovery:** new evidence may extend the seed without losing any historical record; justified corrections need not retain falsehood as 'knowledge'. No requirement to approve harmful requests or to provide physically impossible opportunities.

The desired theorem is an unconditional **real-domain assertion** only if those properties are independently derived from physical law or enforced by a specified system. Avoid asserting `∀ e` permission for every conceivable effect, which contradicts X3 restraint and prevention of destructive acts. Until those derivations are obtained, record this as a target, not as theorem completion.

## 6. Important infinite-domain distinction

A proof `∀ n : Nat` yields a theorem about **every finite iteration**, with no fixed finite cutoff; this is a valuable infinity result. It does NOT construct an actual infinite-energy physical machine, make the next U exist, guarantee that a universe is a simulation, or prove continuous numerical improvement. The `Int` genealogy in `KoruEternalKnowledgeSeed` does prove all-epoch predecessor/successor inside its specified model, but its theorem `no_nat_quality_strictly_improves_at_every_integer_epoch` shows why a nonnegative numerical progress metric cannot strictly increase along every step of an unbounded-below integer lineage. Preserve this constraint instead of silently changing it.
