## Mandatory negative controls
1. **Vacuity:** `all_event_closure_does_not_force_a_successor` demonstrates that the statement "every actual step has X" can hold for an empty step relation and does not guarantee nonterminal physical evolution.
2. **Actual physical terminal model with nonempty lawful earlier steps:** `KoruGenesisPhysicalForcingFiretest.source_refinement_does_not_force_physical_nonstalling` remains a stronger preserved countermodel.
3. **No authentic authorization from shape alone:** `G3KoruShapeAdversarialGate.shape_cannot_by_itself_force_original_six`.
4. **No automatic new witnesses:** `G3InductiveCompleteness.one_missing_step_witness_breaks_completeness`.
5. **No universal W coverage by the Tree's self-model:** `G3TreeCoverage.COVERAGE_IFF_ALIGNED_AND_W_FAITHFUL` is scoped to tree steps; an independently specified external physical event is not automatically one of them.
6. **No energy scalar substitute for Koru:** `G3KoruVsEnergyConservation.energy_conserved_yet_a_soul_is_lost` is a mathematical countermodel.
