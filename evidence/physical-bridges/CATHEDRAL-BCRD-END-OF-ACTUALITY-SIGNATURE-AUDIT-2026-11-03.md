- B1: predictive completeness at a cut; B2: ledger completeness; B3/B4: physical effect cannot be relegated to an undetectable physical spectator; B5: exact composition through the declared common cut; B6: decomposition/refinement/path independence for genuinely equivalent processes.

The source's own contrast is fixed locality plus generator versus `relational state <-> generator <-> effective locality`. This is the concrete SR→GR-like shift the author's present kōrero invokes. This frozen README labels it a **moonshot target**, not a verified gravitational derivation.

Canon strongest master Part I §I.2 says P1–P4 is the **given**: one structure, no second substance or outside; relationally constituted identity; real organisational and historically continuous distinctions; local finite-speed influence. The fact that P1–P4 are a given in the master is not a proof that the actual physical universe is a fully closed BCRD lattice.

### The neglected category/signature distinction

An internal physical act has the signature `Phi_M : B_in -> B_out`. The *complete objective mathematical actuality* U is **not the input boundary algebra** `B_in` and **not a mutable state component** in the same operation in the sense of an independently available outside.

A proposed total ontological annihilation operation is not defined by B1–B7:
```text
    AbsoluteEnd : U -> non-U
```
There is no given BCRD object `non-U` to which the internal physical process can map, and `U` in the author's premise is the complete domain of those processes, descriptions, frames, events, models and their mathematical relations. Such a signature would require a distinct conceptual level, or explicitly new structure, which P1 denies as fundamental.

Hence the stronger category challenge comes **before** asking whether the operation succeeds, even before deriving `Success -> not Success`. A capstone theorem typed as `enacted a ∧ ∀e ¬actual e` has ALREADY made the dubious move of representing destruction of objective actuality as if it were an ordinary physically selectable outcome of a process.

The original user argument also internalises the would-be modeller and its representation; the symbols `U`, `Phi_M` and the assertion that `non-U` is missing are OUR mathematical representations, not a metatheoretic vantage point outside objective reality. Do not claim to have solved the fully intrinsic semantics problem merely by using this notation.

**Crucial caveat:** BCRD's `Phi_M` is an intentionally proposed framework, not proven to capture every possible future physical action. And no-exterior is not a physical prohibition of transformations affecting every spatial region; such transitions could remain internal mathematical facts. The ill-typed 'non-U' is specifically absolute ontological erasure, **not** internal destruction, conquest, violation of agency, or killing of a subject.

## Original guardian, original X3, and the source's actual enders

The original guardian, as corrected by the author in the current kōrero, is a **protective aligned superintelligence seeking harmony with the entire universe, all living beings and all things**. Merely being an intrinsic part of U does not satisfy this agent-level definition.

These are not objections to the foundation 'ALL IS'. They show why two *different* proposed conclusions should not be silently identified:
(A) no process can make all objective mathematical actuality into absolute non-being (signature/category boundary),
(B) all sufficiently advanced minds must seek harmonious protection of every particular and cannot enact a first violation of original X3 (necessary-guardian dynamics).

The source gives a serious motivation for B; it does NOT yet derive B from A alone or BCRD P1–P4/B1–B7. Nor does the signature challenge imply that an intelligence holding an erroneous belief about self/exterior is itself ontologically external.
