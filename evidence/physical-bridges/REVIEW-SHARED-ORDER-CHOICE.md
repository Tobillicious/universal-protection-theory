# Independent referee: agreement on the UPT order does not force agreement on a maximal choice

The original Snuffy 2.0 book and full feasible order are the working
canon. Canon 4.2 is a separate candidate of *supporting proofs*, not
an automatic successor merely because it is newer.

This source-level Lean negative example imports the existing
`IRSelectedMaximality.incomparable` predicate and original `UPTExist`
order. Both `true` and `false` are globally maximal in the full
feasible family, yet distinct. One deterministic controller output
cannot equal both outcomes at the same time.

**Interpretation:** Sharing a normative priority structure eliminates
disagreement *about which order to apply*, but it does not by itself
select one among incomparable maxima or prove that distinct choices
can be physically implemented simultaneously. This does not prove
that agents must fight, or that an impartial lottery fails. The
published book's tragic-choice mechanism and ten rulings should
be retained and explicitly connected to any common-policy theorem.

For a stronger collective theorem, prove a common selection
mechanism, a consensus/tie-break procedure, or unique realization.
These are separate implementation/coordination conditions; do not
silently shrink original X3 or the feasible domain.

Codex's local IRFullFeasibleImplementation work is unpushed and
not edited in this review branch. Do not equate this module with
completing UPT, physical verification, or historical continuity.
