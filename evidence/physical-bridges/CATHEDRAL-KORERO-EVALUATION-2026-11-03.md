## 1. The kōrero's foundational claim, at its strongest form

The transcript's settled thesis is not "existence wins a struggle" — it is that **there is no operation referent for ending existence**: every candidate referent (a boundary, an after, an absence, `U → ∅`) is itself a constituent of what IS, so the question is ill-formed before any answer. This is *stronger than and prior to* the compiled floor `CathedralCapstoneJoins.ender_success_self_refutes`, which proves the thin form `(Success → ¬Success) → ¬Success` and must first be *given* self-negating content.

Draft PR #244 discharges that content for one witnessed definition. The full well-formedness thesis, by contrast, is a typing/semantic discipline on formulations — it lives correctly in the lane documents as guidance, and the corpus is right not to claim it as a compiled theorem. This lane's assessment: the discipline is sound, and its two operational corollaries should be kept symmetric in all future lanes:

- **(a)** Any argument that requires an outside (external observer, external clock, external truth oracle, external ledger) is void as an objection to the ontology.
- **(b)** An *internal*, well-formed threat — first X3 harm among constituents of the one actuality — is **not** dissolvable by the same test. It must be forbidden by physics, metering and enforcement, or not at all. PR #242 is exactly such an internal threat, and the category-error invocation must never be used to close it.

Corollary (b) is the one place where the otherwise excellent category-error discipline could be misused in future, and this document registers it as a standing guard.

## 4. Draft PR #245 — assessment: correct bounded construction; the two-codebook ambiguity is the right frontier

The grounded self-semantics module proves what it claims: grounded facts stable from one step (`grounded_true_at_every_positive_stage`), liar-like positions `pending` at every finite stage, no bivalent liar fixed point, the pending status as the unique negation fixed point, accurate true and false self-reports under an alternative codebook, and equivariance of code and stages under the source web's fixed-point-free symmetry. As a bounded partial-truth construction in existing mathematics, this lane finds it correct and well-scoped, and agrees with the lane's own conclusion that no new primitive is needed for the bounded result.

The two-codebook counterexample (`Meaning(U,C1,p) ≠ Meaning(U,C2,p)`) is honestly promoted to the next exact obligation: derive the interpretation programme from the relational structure, or prove what additional structure suffices. This lane offers three candidate routes, in increasing order of consonance with the existing corpus:

1. **Fixed-point constraint on the programme itself:** require the codebook to be a fixed point of the one-step interpretation map restricted to grounded instructions. `grounded_limit_is_fixed` shows such assignments exist; the obligation becomes deriving the *programme*, not merely a fixed assignment. Necessary but probably insufficient alone.
2. **Structural determination of instruction type:** determine each position's instruction from intrinsic features of its relational profile (e.g., self-loop topology or neighbourhood-separation profile) rather than supplied presentation coordinates. Testable on `balancedWeb`; would make the programme equivariant *by construction* rather than by check.
3. **Identity-by-history for codes (D60/D63 route):** the interpreter's codebook *is* the interpreter's own history — its position in the complete relational structure. Then the two-codebook ambiguity is not an obstacle but two distinct intrinsic histories, i.e. exactly the author's resolution in the kōrero: not one unique interpretation, but **multiple genuine interpretations coexisting as intrinsic articulations of the same whole, with no exterior**. This route requires no uniqueness theorem, converts the counterexample into the theorem's content, and reuses the corpus's own identity machinery. It is this lane's recommendation for the next step in the foundations lane, with route 2 as the constructive test that the determination is non-vacuous.

One caution to carry: symmetry alone cannot select an interpretation (already shown by the two-codebook result), so the target should be stated as *existence of an intrinsically determined grounded codebook*, not uniqueness.
