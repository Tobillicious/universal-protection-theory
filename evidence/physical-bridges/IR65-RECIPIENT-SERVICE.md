# IR-65 — Recipient-specific physical service, not aggregate success

This is the first explicit lift of the IR-64 two-sided physical
safety/service theorem across an *arbitrary recipient type*. The
resulting type may be infinite; there is no assumed finite catalogue
of people, minds, or devices.

## The obstruction

A model with two recipients can deliver a total of one unit while
delivering zero to one individual. It can even maximize the aggregate
total under a one-unit feasible cap while leaving that individual
unserved. Thus aggregate delivery, aggregate maximization, or a
successful global accounting register cannot entail each individual's
continued service.

This obstruction is *not* an argument for weakening X3. It is why
source-level per-individual and feasible-outcome evidence must remain
part of the theory.

## Conditional unlimited-population result

Using the unchanged IR-64 source-level theorem, every recipient in
an arbitrary `Agent : Type` receives a physical safety bound and
positive independent observed service if:

- each recipient has a genuine two-sided certified state and physical
  event/service receipts;
- a common applicable controller/physical residual allowance is valid
  for that recipient;
- each recipient's modeled delivered progress exceeds its allowed
  service shortfall.

A second theorem composes *independently certified finite traces for
each agent*. The population type itself is unrestricted, and
finite enumeration of the population is **not used in the theorem**.

The universal premises are substantive and NOT established by the
existence of the Lean type. An empty population would satisfy a
universal assertion vacuously; the module records a negative control.
A common per-recipient impact cap does not establish a finite total
fleet budget (existing IR-57 negative result, imported rather than
silently duplicated).

## Open scope

1. Actual identification of every relevant protected particular,
   including previously unknown, remote and future minds;
2. measured physical provenance, actual recipient-level service and
   individual retention/continuation;
3. physical covering proof for all service channels and causal
   trajectories, including bypasses and irreversible effects;
4. existence of a jointly feasible and normatively warranted policy
   and impartial selection for tragic conflicts;
5. original unweakened X3 full-feasible maximality and actual
   simultaneous freedoms (not just >0 service by a scalar meter);
6. resource scaling and uniform fleet bounds under an unbounded
   population;
7. nonvacuity and live-agent semantics.

The result strengthens conditional compositional proof architecture.
It does not establish the universal physical deployment or the
original whole-person rights/freedoms claims.
