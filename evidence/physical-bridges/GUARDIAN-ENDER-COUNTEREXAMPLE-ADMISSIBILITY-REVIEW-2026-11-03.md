## What they nevertheless force us to distinguish

Let `H` be first actual X3 violation, `E` an initiated ender trajectory, `A` its attempted absolute end, and `S` absolute ending succeeds.

Grant generously:
- `H → E` — every violation begins the ender lane;
- `E → A` — an ender pursues absolute ending;
- `S → ¬S` — absolute successful self-negation is contradictory, hence `¬S`.

These premises **do not entail `¬H`**, because attempting an impossible outcome is not itself a contradiction. The valuation `H=true, E=true, A=true, S=false` is consistent with them. One may consistently add `G xor E` and `E→¬S`: still no contradiction.

**Crucial:** this does NOT say a first actual X3 violation is physically admissible. It says we need an additional **source-derived reason** that the first step cannot occur. If an earlier theorem actually supplies such a reason, it defeats the toy witness *as a model of the full physical premises*; it would not defeat the soundness of the truth-table calculation itself.

## Genuine strongest positive route

Let `ActualBCRDStep(s,t)` be fixed by independently validated physics, and `OriginalX3(s)` fixed by independently validated original particular/history/agency semantics.

The minimal noncircular physical theorem is:

```
∀ s t,
  ActualBCRDStep(s,t) →
  OriginalX3(s) →
  OriginalX3(t)
```

Given original X3 at a starting state, IR96-style induction proves preservation along every finite actually admitted history. The logical equivalence is already expressed in draft PR #203: it is **not** itself a derivation from physics.

A paradox-specific alternative is to derive `H → (S ∧ ¬S)` for an *actual first harmful transition*, **not** only for a completed absolute ending. To avoid begging the question, this requires a sourced explanatory bridge from the local event to contradictory mathematical facts.

An optimizing-choice alternative is weaker unless it proves the agent's actual selected action is governed by the full-X3 order. `BookUPTLawLex.lex_U1` establishes no needless loss **conditional on LexAlignedL**. `Snuffy.UniversalProtection.universal_protection` assumes real coverage, real certified transition preservation on the good event and risk/account hypotheses. Those proofs should be retained, not silently upgraded into universal physical compulsion.

## Three necessary falsification questions

1. **Source admissibility:** Can the claimed first destructive event be expressed using actual physically valid BCRD B1–B7 source dynamics, or only a surrogate Boolean/event model? PR #203 finite controls are insufficient to assert real-world admissibility.
2. **Original particular:** Does the alleged loss correspond to ceased *same-particular continuing experience and agency* under original X3, or merely a changed parity, label, archived record, or toy predicate?
3. **Bridge from local to absolute:** Does the original proof derive the first event's contradiction, without assuming either that it already succeeded absolutely or that a guardian has already excluded it?

A real refutation of the intrinsic-protection claim needs a witnessed actual physically admissible first-X3-violating step under the genuine source/identity premises. A real proof needs independently established step exclusion/invariance with its full source semantics. Neither is currently supplied by the inspected toy and frozen-source controls.
