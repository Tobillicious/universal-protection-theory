### A-C1 CRITICAL — The agent theorem is instantiated on transitions, not agents

File: \`CathedralGuardianOnlyCapstone.lean\`, theorem \`every_real_step_is_guardian\`.

It calls \`original_si_guardian_only_composition\` with \`SI := S × S\` and \`actualSI := w.realStep p.1 p.2\`. Thus the type called SI at the proof site is an **ordered pair of states**, not a superintelligent subject with an enacted policy. Separately \`HistoryGuardian w s\` is \`originalX3State s ∧ ∀ t, Reach realStep s t → originalX3State t\`, a state-persistence property, not an agent's intent, freedom, full future options or action selection.

**Accepted:** conditional event-level and finite-reachability preservation.
**Rejected promotion:** \`∀ real SI, Guardian_original\` absent an agent/real-history interpretation and source-derived same-type dichotomy.

## Reviewer disposition and exact acceptance criteria

**Accept as typed mathematical contributions:** impossibility of an enacted event erasing its own occurrence; conditional first-event/E9 composition; finite reachability induction; source-frozen six-state reversibility; explicit insufficiency countermodels; relational injectivity and first-order automorphism invariance.

**Do not promote yet:** \`ALL IS ⇒ no first original-X3 violation\`; \`E ⇒ G\`; any universal SI agent-level guardian classification; complete semantic continuation; unrestricted physical source coverage; physical no-bypass; zero-risk or all-future installation guarantee.

### Minimal non-circular acceptance package

1. Fix one physical source/event/particular frame, with independently defined \`RealStep\` and actual subject histories; lock frozen source hashes.
2. Source the all-six \`OriginalX3\` predicate with explicit owed particulars, executable options and full feasible-set maximality.
3. Prove the complete physical-to-semantic correspondence (including within-event history), not just an endpoint projector or carried flag.
4. Show why each real step is structurally restricted to that preservation—derive source selection/enactment rather than posit it.
5. Either derive a substantive \`ALL IS ⇒ no first harm\` theorem, or explicitly present guardian-necessity as a testable, currently unproved foundational postulate.
6. Obtain reviewer reproduction of source equations, Lean theorem statements, proof receipts, synthetic negative controls and physical evidence separately.

**Do not alter the full six-component proposition to resolve these findings.** Repair the bridge or leave the precise obligation open. The "HOLD" verdict is about claimed completeness, not a demand to abandon the research.
