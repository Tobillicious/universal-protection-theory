## Minimal implication test

Let G = realised guardian policy, E = ender policy initiated, H = first X3 harm, A = attempt at absolute ending, S = absolute ending succeeds.

Grant, for the test, the **stronger-than-necessary** premises:
1. `G xor E` (exhaustive exclusive classification);
2. `H → E`;
3. `E → A` (an ender attempts the absolute target);
4. `S → ¬S`, hence `¬S` (the trivial paradox);
5. `E → ¬S` (every ender fails absolutely, even without relying on historical hazard conditions).

These do **not** imply `G` or `¬H`. Witness: `G=false, E=true, H=true, A=true, S=false`.

The witness represents an attempted ender that first harms a particular and then fails to end mathematics. It is a **propositional** countermodel, not an empirical BCRD universe. The exact 2^5 truth-table checker is `check_guardian_ender_paradox.py`.
