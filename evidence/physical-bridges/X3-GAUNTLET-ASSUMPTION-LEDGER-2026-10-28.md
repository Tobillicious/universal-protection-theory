## Three precise logical separations

1. A reduced channel/noise model can destroy *local* historical information
   while emitted bath modes or an independent witness preserve it. A proof of
   recoverability from the complete admitted inventory is a different claim.
2. A lower bound on delayed reconstruction expense is **not** itself a proof
   of safe action availability, consent, or continuing-identity preservation.
3. An observational viable-policy existence theorem is **not** a proof of
   executable hardware policy, nor of universal maximally free coexistence.

### Exact installation-verification obligations

1. **Actual transition completeness.** Show that EVERY consequential physical
   change—independent of the digital log, including common-mode faults,
   passive energy release, sensor/actuator faults and third-party effects—is
   represented by an admitted source transition or a separately charged
   residual budget. Theorems quantifying over `InstalledStep` or
   `WitnessedStep` do not establish that every real-world step belongs to
   either relation.
2. **Physical effect correspondence and units.** Measure or derive the
   per-particular harmful increment and prove the IR-117 inequality with
   compatible physical units. IR-118's numeric `landed` represents
   an abstract additive burden, not automatically joules, biological harm,
   loss of capacity, or a relational-history invariant.
3. **Closure and backlog integrity.** Show closing admissions is effective
   at the final physical apply boundary. Both `inFlight` and `pending`
   must be complete; already-committed effects do not vanish on a hold.
   Validate latent stored-energy channels separately using IR-80.
4. **Historical witness authenticity.** Demonstrate that IR-26's abstract
   event chain is causally bound to the real particular's ongoing physical
   process. Event replay, origin-token copying and matching observations
   are weaker than that binding.
5. **Subject detection and duty continuity.** Identify the actual intended
   domain of minds. Prove that admission/recognition does not miss
   unregistered or newly appearing subjects, and that deleting a mutable
   record cannot erase their independent protection rights (IR-116).
6. **Authorization and full agency.** Validate freely authorized actions,
   independent choice, right to evolve, safe re-entry and sustained service.
   IR-117's `executable` and `required` are physically supplied predicates,
   not evidence of the original complete maximal freedom order.
7. **Robust reachable observation classes.** Establish the IR-113 common
   viable-action condition under delayed, faulty sensing and heterogeneous
   disturbances. A controller that merely exists by classical choice is
   not an algorithm/hardware implementation.
8. **Physics and universality.** Tie the installation and subject ontology
   to a specified, independently testable BCRD physical model, then justify
   the claimed scope across real possible systems and optimizers. The
   recovered October 24 area-activation/outward-transport gaps (IR-75/76)
   remain separate open universal physical joins.
