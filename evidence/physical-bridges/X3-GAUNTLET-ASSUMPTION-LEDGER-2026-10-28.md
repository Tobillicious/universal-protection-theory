## Three precise logical separations

1. A reduced channel/noise model can destroy *local* historical information
   while emitted bath modes or an independent witness preserve it. A proof of
   recoverability from the complete admitted inventory is a different claim.
2. A lower bound on delayed reconstruction expense is **not** itself a proof
   of safe action availability, consent, or continuing-identity preservation.
3. An observational viable-policy existence theorem is **not** a proof of
   executable hardware policy, nor of universal maximally free coexistence.
