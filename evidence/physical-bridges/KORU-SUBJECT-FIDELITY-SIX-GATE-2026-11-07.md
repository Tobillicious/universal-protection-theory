### Structural interface, not a disguised safety predicate

The independent subject interpretation is a `ParticularRelations Nat P` with six independently chosen predicates. The source is a `SixOperatorSourceDuties` over all operator-indexed first-order expressions, including nested operators. The candidate `carrier : P → Index` and role map `roles : Index → Fin 4 → Index` are separately specified.

`WholeSourceFidelity r d roles carrier` requires every **individual tag** of the subject's intrinsic six-field relational web to be **truth-value equivalent** to its complete-operator source tag at every W stage. It **does not assert that any tag is true**. The hostile theorem explicitly satisfies fidelity and injective carrier distinctness while source-authorization remains false and complete original X3 is false. Therefore, unlike the earlier disallowed `hAuthenticReflections ↔ every owed X3` shortcut, the **fidelity criterion is not itself a positive protection guarantee**; source-origin truth is an additional independent positive premise.

This is a mathematical model-interface for externally validating physical/semantic meaning, **not** yet a physical verification of that meaning. A pure syntactic pullback of source formulas is an algebraic example, never by itself proof of valid individual autonomy or ongoing life.

### A sharper next experimental/semantic gate

For any particular `p` and stage `n`, under independently established positive complete source reports, if one genuine original-X3 component fails, `original_violation_iff_exact_source_subject_mismatch` identifies a concrete tag whose measured/validated subject predicate disagrees with the operator source. It cannot obtain the needed correspondence by redefining a failed semantic duty as a source truth, or by omitting the event/subject.
