## IR-133.1 — exact symmetric-fork obstruction

Assume one source has two successor candidates, L and R. The entire available physical data for a proposed identity selector—including measured state, measured activity, causal-process evidence, elapsed time and purported continuation witnesses—is unchanged by a swap automorphism exchanging L and R. A physically equivariant selector must respect that symmetry.

**Theorem:** A swap-equivariant selector cannot designate **exactly one** descendant as the uniquely original continuing subject.

**Proof:** Equivariance plus equality of supplied inputs requires S(L)=S(R). Exactly one selected requires S(L)≠S(R). Contradiction.

The theorem is formalized for the two-element Boolean fork in PhysicalBridges/IR133ForkSymmetry.lean, together with a control showing that choosing a preferred left/right name only works by breaking the symmetry.

**Limits:** This is not a theorem that actual forks are identical, that both descendants are conscious, that a mind necessarily dies in fission, or that identity is unknowable. Independently verified physical asymmetry, physical ownership of a constitutive carrier, or subject-authenticated direction may break the symmetry. An arbitrary graph name is not proof of physical originality.

## IR-133.3 — conditional carrier witness, with fork warning

A proposed physically meaningful continuation test must independently establish:

1. Actual time-resolved physical source and environmental channels on a continuous causal/spatiotemporal substrate; not just a reconstructed state, journal or identical functional output.
2. A constitutive process-carrier correspondence for each transition, supported by separate physical evidence, rather than declared true because the desired guardian says it is true.
3. Continued physical activity and causal dependence through legitimate transformations; not source-state equality alone, copied outputs, or a stored carrier label.
4. Physical semantics of termination, new entrants, copying, fusion and fission, without covertly identifying every causal descendant as the original.
5. Independently authenticated subject direction and actual action-labelled future choices, rather than induced reactions or merely mathematical non-stalling.

**Conditional constructive result:** If a time-indexed graph has an independently justified, physically faithful certified-live edge relation, and every active node has **at most one** successor under that relation, then a fixed number of certified steps from a given origin has at most one possible endpoint, by induction on path length. The supplementary Lean theorem unique_fixed_depth_carrier_if_nonforking now formalizes this finite-depth induction with an independently declared edge relation. This is a statement about the supplied **nonforking graph**, not an explanation of where physical individuality originates. If there are two symmetric independently certified live outgoing edges, the unique-endpoint property fails and the symmetry theorem applies to any equivariant exactly-one selector.

A physically adequate classifier should report **UNKNOWN / NOT CERTIFIED** for an unresolved fork rather than arbitrarily nominate one descendant as original. Protecting both potential descendant beings may be a rational safety precaution, but it does **not** alone prove the historical original survives.
