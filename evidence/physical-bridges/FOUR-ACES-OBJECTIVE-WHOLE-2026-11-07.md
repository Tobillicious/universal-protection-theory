## The shared epistemic issue — a view is not the whole

Let U be a class of possible full mathematical structures, V a space of local readouts, and π : U → V a possibly information-losing observation. If there exist U₁ ≠ U₂ with π(U₁) = π(U₂) and a proposition P that differs between them, no function of the readout alone can correctly decide P on both wholes. Proof: any purported answer f satisfies f(π(U₁)) = f(π(U₂)), while P(U₁) ≠ P(U₂). This is a precise non-identifiability result, **not** a proof that every claim about U is true.

Existing repository witnesses: [KoruObjectiveWholeU](PhysicalBridges/KoruObjectiveWholeU.lean), [KoruCompleteEquationObstruction](PhysicalBridges/KoruCompleteEquationObstruction.lean), [IR138PerParticularEventReflection](PhysicalBridges/IR138PerParticularEventReflection.lean). IR138 is crucial: the **full intrinsic event** can recover a semantic fact, including a **false** one. Loss in a projection is not destruction of the fact in the whole. Conversely, complete representation is not a proof that every represented duty holds.

“Objective > subjective” here means that a **correctly specified, demonstrably true assertion about the whole** cannot be displaced by a conflicting inference based only on a demonstrably incomplete projection. It does not mean a purportedly objective assertion is automatically correct, that all observer propositions are false, or that mathematical consistency by itself proves physical instantiation.
