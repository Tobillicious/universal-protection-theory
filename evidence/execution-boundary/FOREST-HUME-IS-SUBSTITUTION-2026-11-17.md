# Hume's Tree of Life and Endless Knowledge — typed IS substitutions

## The generative insight

The author's proposed fulcrum begins at an independently meaningful **IS**.
Choose an *existing* individual `p : P`, a stage `n : Nat`, the objective
whole `U`, or Guardian `G`. Different typed instances may describe the
same actuality, but substitution is legal only through an explicit typing and
correspondence map; `p`, `n`, `U`, `G` are not interchangeable Lean terms.

General form: a domain `D`, actual states `W`, actual transition relation
`realStep : W → W → Prop`, owed particulars `P`, `owed : P → Prop`, a
domain-specific interpretation `read`, and authentic source predicate
families. Ethics problems become mathematical *questions and duties* at this
interface. Deduction of their truth requires noncircular semantic premises.

## Four root substitutions and their obligations

| IS root | Mathematical carrier | Example question | Faithfulness needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A person **is** | `p : P`, `owed p` | A requested act affects a person's freedom | Current identity, authentic agency, full six-field correspondence |
| A stage **is** | `n : Nat`, history / `W` | An authorization was withdrawn before effect | Ordered actual events, pre/post and revocation-time evidence |
| A universe **is** | `U` whole source | Two views encode same particular differently | Full signature transport, no omitted affected source roles |
| Guardian **is** | `G` as intended invariant of whole | Can a protected action be executed under HOLD? | Actual effect enforcement and proved source-to-executor mapping |

## The forest rule

For *any* ethical source question `e` with independently defined meaning,
provide a faithful embedding `interpret(e)`, preserve subject distinctions and
show every actual effect has a corresponding complete protected transition.
Reuse `ExternalJointEffectRefinement.independently_covered_events_satisfy_original_six`
once `hCoverage` and all six `hMeaning` components are independently
established. The schema handles arbitrary cases by quantification; it does
not prove each independent premise merely by using a variable.

## R5 connection, without weakening the physical boundary

Authority-owned execution is one model of *enactment*: an untrusted proposal
is not a durable effect. For each instantiated ethics question specify the
actual effect owner, allowed operations, forbidden paths, HOLD precedence and
observable outcome. The protected effect paths must be exhausted by measured
independent evidence, not by defining `realStep` as "approved events".

Unsolved: exhaustive actual effect coverage, genuine subject/domain semantics,
off-host custody and independent acceptance. The tree grows by typed,
independently witnessed joins, not by erasing these obligations.
