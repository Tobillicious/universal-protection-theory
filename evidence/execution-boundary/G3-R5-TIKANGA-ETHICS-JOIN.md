## Join to authority-owned R5

An ethical or Tīkanga operator may give *subject-specific reasons* to allow,
withhold, revise or refuse an effect. It does not by itself supply a kernel
capability or permission to issue a protected operation. The existing
`AuthorityOwnedMarkerService` remains the sole effect owner for the bounded
fixture; its existing HOLD and fixed-operation checks remain intact.

For any eventual real-world expansion, map each exact, sourced operator to:
1. Its authenticated source, jurisdiction/context, affected particulars, and
   independently warranted cultural interpretation.
2. All six original X3 duties and any more specific obligation the operator
   demands. Never collapse the family into generic `is_safe` or maximum score.
3. An independently identified actual event, current rights and revocation,
   effective HOLD ordering, recorded decision, and protected effect outcome.
4. An auditable mapping to `realStep`, `read`, `owed`, `carrier`, and
   each field of `AuthenticSixReflections` without circular definitions.

This mapping is a source-and-evidence task, **not** permission to invent the
complete named mathematical Tīkanga operator family. Genuine tikanga
interpretations must be reviewed by appropriate knowledge holders rather
than inferred from a software label.
