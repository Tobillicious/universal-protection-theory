## XIII.1 The floor: physics presupposes mathematics

mathematical structure, so every physical agent is a structure within
mathematics, and its acts are mathematical facts. An absolute ender's victory
would be a mathematical fact whose content is the nonexistence of mathematical
facts. `ender_success_self_refutes` (kernel-checked, axiom-free) records the
trivial paradox in one line: a success whose own content is the negation of
that success cannot occur. On a cyclic history there is no final phase on
which the ending could be written. This is the reading of the edition's
self-grounding capstone (Part VII's nontrivial evaluation witness): the
structure grounds its own continuation, so nothing outside mathematics stands
for an ending to stand on. The author's supplied route — the experienced
matterspacetime as a subjective view from a position and orientation in the
lattice, the particular being the co-ordinate rather than the substrate — is
recorded with the integration bank of the canon working master; it makes the
only real death the severance of a perspective from its own history, which the
complete-ledger structure of Parts III and VI forbids.
