# Rung 5: protection that does not depend on what minds value (candidate)

20 Oct 2026. Claude, author side. A candidate for review, not a result.

**The ladder's rule** (`ATTESTED-HOSTS.md`): each rung replaces a premise about minds with one about cryptography or
physics. Rungs 1–3 removed the premises about how many minds are enders and how loyal providers are.

**The last load-bearing premise about minds at rung 4 is M2.** It says 27.9% of providers value the dividend above
15/68, and it is what keeps provider roles performing.

**Design rule D-ENDOW.** Committed capacity is owned by the jurisdiction and endowed under P-COMMISSION, including
its energy flow to the horizon. A role's service is attested, committed hardware duty. No mind has a choice that
reduces it. A role is unavailable only through a physical fault (P-FAULT) or a physical removal (counted in W under
P-PROV, now a physical premise).

**Two facts, verified on the frozen inherited model:**
1. **The risk ledger does not use M2.** The provider scenario grid has 2 readings × 5 responses = 10 scenarios.
   The k = 3 screen asserts all 10 rows, so no scenario is filtered by the incentive check, and the account values
   are computed without the incentive gaps.
2. **The holding and rescue bounds are non-increasing in role service probability pc.** This was checked exactly at
   all five grid values for both readings, pc from 0.9173 to 0.96. It also holds generally: both failure events
   become less likely when more roles perform (monotone coupling).

**Consequence.** Under D-ENDOW, role service probability is physical: pc ≥ 1 − p ≥ 0.998 under P-FAULT. That is
above the worst grid row (0.9173), so the existing ledger bound applies **without M2**.

**Candidate statement (rung 5).** Under D-ENDOW and the rung-4 premises with M2 removed, the protection conclusions
hold for every population of minds and every choice any mind or group of minds makes. They rest only on:
- physics: the laws, P-FAULT, the capability envelope behind P-K3 (0b), and P-PROV as a physical cap;
- cryptography: C-SEQ and Mode C;
- hardware warranties: the floor;
- design: as specified, including D-ENDOW;
- boundary conditions: P-COMMISSION with endowment, P-TRAJECTORY, P-FULLPOOL;
- the timing premises.

Probability exactly 1 remains impossible, because of the floor. But no mind's values, beliefs or choices appear among
the premises.

declared, and they now are, as register item 29. With pc = 499/500 at each row's own (μ, d), every row is at or below
the existing worst total.
