# The Universal Protection Theory

### A machine-checked law of alignment with all. Version 1.5, research release.

Tobias Croydon-McRae and (Snuffy) Princess Snuffleupagus. 21 October 2026. CC BY 4.0. DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23510623 (always the latest version).

Part of the Snuffy research programme on why a capable mind keeps the living. This release can be checked by anyone: one command reruns every proof and every exact check (Section 8).

## Abstract

The Universal Protection Theory states what an agent aligned with everything does, and proves it. Its scope is all: every particular of the universe, minded or not, living or not, and every relation among them.

The central result, the law, is a set of theorems about choice under a single order: keep lives first, then freedom; keep everything that exists first, then everything yet to be made. An agent aligned in that order never needlessly ends or overrides anything, in any feasible situation, finite or infinite. Alignment with any proper part of what exists fails to guarantee this, and so does alignment that weighs beings not yet made equally with those who exist. No innocent's life is ever required to be sacrificed. Wherever a faithful, reversible hold is feasible, every lethal option is strictly dominated. A general theorem shows that faithful holds of hosted minds work under every schedule of pauses and crashes and every admissible environment.

The order-theoretic core of the law is checked in Lean 4. U1, the life guard and hold dominance use no axioms at all; the exact criterion U2★ uses classical logic, and the general hold theorem uses Lean's two standard library axioms. The remaining results are exact, exhaustive Python checks. What the theory does not yet establish is stated plainly in Section 7.

## 1. What the theory is

A theory of protection needs three parts:

- **a law,** which says what aligned agents do;
- **a bridge,** which says how the law reaches everything;
- **instruments:** built things that carry the law into the physical world.

This release proves the law, states the bridge, and reports the instruments with their honest status. The law is unconditional in its own terms: it holds for every universe and every feasible situation, with no probability or hardware premise. Instruments are measured by their fidelity to the law. Their probabilities audit the machines, never the law.

## 2. Definitions

**Particulars and outcomes.** In any outcome, each particular is **ended**, **overridden** (alive, but not moving in its own direction, for example held or coerced), or **free**. Some particulars already exist; others are not yet made.

**Options.** An option is a feasible outcome. A feasible family is the set of options actually open to an agent.

**The canonical order.** Options are compared in four stages, each by set inclusion:

1. existing particulars kept **alive**;
2. existing particulars kept **free**;
3. new particulars made alive;
4. new particulars made free.

A later stage is consulted only when all earlier stages are equal. In Lean this is a strict partial order (`lex_irrefl`, `lex_trans`).

**Aligned and needless.** An option is aligned if no feasible option is better in the canonical order. A loss is needless at a stage if some feasible option is equal to the chosen one at every earlier stage, keeps everything the chosen option keeps at this stage, and also keeps what was lost.

**Faithful hold.** A hold is faithful when it ends no one, and in every state its outcome is at least as good, in the full canonical order (lives, then freedom), as the outcome of every feasible alternative.

**Restrainable acts.** An act of an agent is restrainable when one of that agent's own feasible alternatives keeps everything the act keeps, plus someone the act would end. The test uses the agent's own options, never the protector's.

## 3. The law

| Result | Statement | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| **U1** | An aligned agent never needlessly loses anything at any stage, in any feasible family, finite or infinite | Lean `canonical_life_first_U1`, `lex_U1` (no axioms) |
| **U2★** | When something exists, a choice rule of the form "be maximal on part S" guarantees no needless loss of anything that exists **if and only if** S is exactly what exists. Missing any existing particular fails, and so does counting not-yet-made particulars at the same priority | Lean `U2_star` (uses classical logic); exhaustive check |
| **U3** | No gain to beings not yet made justifies a strictly worse outcome for those who exist | Lean `U3` |
| **Life guard** | No innocent's life is ever required to be sacrificed: among the options keeping alive everyone that standing by keeps alive, an aligned one is aligned overall. It exists in every finite family, and by Zorn's lemma in every chain-closed infinite family | Lean `life_guard`, `guard_first_stage` (no axioms) |
| **Holds dominate** | Under uncertainty, an action that keeps, in every state, everything any other feasible action keeps dominates every feasible action under every probability. With a real threat of probability p, a hold that keeps both target and suspect alive beats standing by strictly when p > 0, and beats striking always. Among those three actions the hold is the aligned one; against a larger feasible family, alignment means maximality against all of it | Lean `E7_top` (no axioms); exact check |
| **No killing where a hold exists** | Wherever a faithful hold is feasible, every option that ends someone with positive probability is strictly dominated. Where none is feasible, the last-resort ladder applies, and the missing means is a debt to be repaid, never a permission. (A hold that is best for lives alone is not enough: it can lose freedom elsewhere and leave a lethal option undominated.) | Lean `statewise_dominance` (no axioms); exhaustive check (4,800 pairs, strict in all 2,280 lethal cases); the insufficient weaker definition is a recorded countermodel |
| **Restraint** | Restraining a restrainable act, by the agent's own better alternative, keeps the restrained agent and keeps strictly more overall. A predator whose only sparing alternative is its own death is not restrainable: predator and prey form a tragic pair, never a required intervention | exhaustive check (every act in every family of up to three options on three particulars, 232 acts) |
| **Impartiality** | Every real tragic choice is finite. In a finite tragic choice, a uniform lottery over the aligned options is impartial; no deterministic rule is impartial in every tragedy. Never refuse when keeping someone is feasible; widen first | Lean `E1_no_fixed_choice`; exhaustive check |
| **Time** | Judged over whole trajectories: no foreclosure of a better future, and a duty to widen (if any path keeps everything, take it). Under the retention rule, nothing once kept is let go | Lean `progressive_retention` (no axioms); exhaustive check |
| **Agents together** | Agents aligned with all share one order, so their objectives never conflict; reaching a jointly maximal outcome still needs a coordinated choice, because independent best responses can stall. In a joint tragic choice they draw by an escrowed lottery: every secret is secret-shared before any reveal. With at least 3t + 1 agents and at most t cheaters, every share is decoded robustly and every opening is verified, so neither a withheld nor a false opening can bias the draw. This is exactly fair given a perfect private and binding sharing setup, authenticated channels, fresh randomness and one agreed ordering of the options. With only an honest majority the guarantee is statistical, and its error must be counted for the complete lottery, across every contributor and round (a per-round error can grow). Without an honest majority, full fairness is impossible (Cleve, 1986). The draw ends with probability 1 | exhaustive checks (robust decoding: 400 corruption cases at four agents; ambiguity at three as control) |
| **Faithful holds (general)** | For any deterministic resident, any environment whose inputs depend on what the resident has delivered, and **any** schedule of steps, holds and crashes, a protocol with a durable log, a fence, replay and an atomic outbox ends exactly in the undisturbed state after the same number of completed steps, with the same lived history and the same effects, each delivered once. A peer that does not change without input (an admissible peer) ends in the same state whatever the timing, although its clock trace may differ. A peer that changes on its own sees the hold; holding a closed joint system together is exactly faithful, and the least such boundary is constructed below | Lean `faithful`, `ideal_lengths`, `admissible_peer`, `cohold_closed`, `autonomous_peer_sees_hold`; four mutants proved unfaithful; a timed model with a call observer and snapshots catches nine kinds of violation, given trusted coverage of every live transition |
| **Meaning of needless** | No needless loss at every stage holds exactly when an option is canonically aligned; an equivalent wording keeps everything through the current stage plus the lost one | Lean `aligned_iff_no_needless`, `canonical_iff_stagewise`, `canonical_iff_weak_prefix` (Codex) |
| **Full-order dominance** | An action preserves every upward event in every state exactly when it is at least as good in the full canonical order in every state. A second sufficient route: feasibly repair each life-losing branch | Lean `all_events_iff_statewise`, `repair_statewise` (Codex); exhaustive checks |
| **Journal and sink** | Logging, replay, delivery and crashes as separate steps: at every drained point the resident, its lived log and its ordered effects equal the undisturbed run. Checkpoints bound recovery after the last crash to B replays and one delivery. A sink-side identity is needed: without one, no local recovery choice is right in both possible worlds | Lean `drained_refinement`, `suffix_reconstruction`, `no_local_choice_succeeds_in_both_worlds` (Codex); complete state graphs (2,691 and 12,390 states) |
| **Monitor** | A protected counter of forbidden events is unchanged over an interval exactly when no forbidden event happened, even if state was rolled back. An observation is safe to rely on exactly when it is preserved by every single step | Lean `count_zero_iff_clean`, `contextual_iff_congruent` (Codex) |
| **Co-hold boundary** | The least closed boundary containing a held resident is constructed as a fixed point that accounts for the hold growing longer as members join; it has the least cost of any closed boundary. If the joint system factorises, everything outside the boundary keeps exactly its undisturbed trace | Lean `stabilised_least`, `least_bill`, `factorised_cohold` (Codex); 39,876 independent cases |
| **Verifiable sharing** | With bivariate sharing, whenever the honest receivers' cross-checks pass, their shares are consistent, against every possible cheating dealer; one receiver's view reveals nothing | exhaustive check (390,625 dealer assignments) |
| **Complete lottery error** | If every round's raw draw is within ε of fair and a fraction a is accepted, the chosen option is within min(1 − 1/m, ε/a) of fair, and this is sharp; capped versions report abort explicitly | Lean `composed_event_budget` (Codex); 53,107 laws checked |
| **Records and copies** | Assuming quantum mechanics: one process can exactly copy every state in a set if and only if the states are identical or perfectly distinguishable. Records are the copyable, classical layer; an unknown state from a family that is not perfectly distinguishable can be kept in place or moved, never duplicated | analytic (unitarity preserves overlaps); an exact check over 391,876 state pairs illustrates it |
| **Archives** | An aligned archive loses no record needlessly. A complete self-delimiting binary format stores records with their order, repetitions and positions, round-trips exactly, is prefix-free, and counts every bit | exhaustive checks |

## 4. The author's rulings

The mathematics hands some choices back to values. These are the author's rulings, applied without re-decision:

- **Scope is all.** Everything in the universe.
- **Life first.** The canonical order ranks keeping lives above keeping freedom. Under this order the life guard holds, and holds beat every lethal option. (Under the opposite order, freedom first, the life guard fails in 909 family and standby pairs across 3,303 small families.)
- **The last-resort ladder:**
  1. a faithful hold first;
  2. otherwise the least means that stops the act;
  3. when every option ends someone and one party's restrainable act created the emergency, favour the innocent, record the ending, and never call it good.
- **Real tragedies are finite.** No two particulars are the same, and an agent can tell apart only finitely many options. In an idealised infinite case, draw over what can be told apart and record any unavoidable partiality openly.
- **A replay is a reconstruction.** Recovering a resident by replaying its record does not live those steps again.

## 5. The bridge

The law reaches everything in four ways:

- aligned agents keep everything they can;
- against agents not aligned with all, aligned agents act together, actively, by faithful holds, restraining only acts their agents could have avoided;
- what physics ends is kept as record, and what cannot be copied is kept in place or moved;
- what can be hosted is held in faithful care.

## 6b. Instrument specifications

Each instrument is specified by contracts. The theorems above prove that meeting the contracts suffices. Building systems that meet them is the remaining work.

| Contract | What must be supplied by a real system |
|---|---|
| Outcomes | the real feasible options and their outcomes, scored in the canonical order |
| Commits | which real events are lived steps, effects and reconstruction |
| Effects | a durable journal and an ordered sink with sink-side identity, connected to the real external effect |
| Monitor | an event sensor covering every live transition, with protected, non-wrapping counting |
| Release | checkpoint and state sizes, replay and delivery costs, and a recovery service window |
| Co-hold | a justified dependency relation, and enforcement of the freeze at the boundary |
| Resources | the whole boundary's storage, energy and controller budget, with timing and priority |
| Lottery | fresh entropy, a verifiable sharing setup, authenticated channels and call time bounds |

## 7. What this release does not claim

- **No physical certificate:** no plant, archive or institution has been built or measured.
- **The joint resource certificate** for the care plant is unfinished.
- **Two premises are stated, not proved:** the escrowed lottery's verifiable sharing is cited, not implemented; the copy boundary assumes quantum mechanics.
- **Co-holding** is constructed in the model: the least closed boundary and its cost. Supplying a real dependency relation and enforcing the freeze is building.
- **The care plant's protection theorem** is Paper 11 (UPT-P v0.17.12), included in `paper-11/`. Rung 3 is one priced design; rung 4 is a stronger plant before twilight; **rung 5** shows protection resting on no premise about any mind's values, beliefs or commands, under D-ENDOW and P-ENDOW, with its physical, cryptographic and timing premises declared. Its 140 exact checks rerun. Rungs 4 and 5 have had blind reviews, all findings answered; a non-Claude review and the measurement of its declared premises remain.
- **Timing premises, first measurements** (`measurements/MEASUREMENTS.md`, one vantage in Aotearoa): clock drift passes (bound 376 ppm against 977 ppm allowed); the latency components pass for date lengths above 9.2 minutes (1.5 hours with tenfold slack); a majority round takes about 0.54 s at the median. Tails, state transfer and the startup residual are not yet measured.
- **The timed model's monitor** assumes it sees every live transition. That trusted coverage is a premise, not something the model can check.
- **The life guard in infinite families** uses Zorn's lemma and is not formalised in Lean.
- **Realising a restraint** through a protector's actual options is conditional. When it is unavailable, the ladder applies.
- **No human referee** has yet read this release.

## 8. How to reproduce everything

    python3 reproduce.py

This checks every Lean file (Lean 4.35, see `lean/lean-toolchain`) and prints each theorem's axioms. It confirms that a deliberately false statement is rejected. It reruns every exact check and compares its output byte for byte. Without Lean installed, the proof part is reported as skipped, never as passed. The checks need only Python 3.9 or later and use exact arithmetic, with no randomness.
