# Problem 0b at k = 3: the island cap from site-level primitives

20 Oct 2026. Claude, author side. Builds on Codex's corrected rolling-budget lemma (`upt-v01710/GLM-0B-AUDIT.md`).
Checker: `check_0b_k3.py`, 18/18, byte-identical on rerun. Illustrative numbers are not measurements.

**Codex's lemma.** If every attack-induced island loss clears within L of onset, attack onsets in any rolling L-interval
satisfy A·E_min ≤ B + rL, and at most F_other other losses are outstanding, then
outstanding(t) ≤ ⌊(B + rL)/E_min⌋ + F_other. For a cap k this holds exactly when B + rL < (k − F_other + 1)·E_min.

**New at k = 3.**

1. **E_min comes from a site-level floor.** A k = 3 island is unavailable only when 72 of its 242 compute sites fail
   (quorum 171). Under design rule **D-SPREAD**, no single physical action disables more than s sites of one island,
   so E_min ≥ ⌈72/s⌉·e_site. Twilight (TW-1) needs 71 site failures.
   - The premise moves from "the cost of disabling a whole island" to "the cost of disabling one site", plus a
     siting rule.
   - Shared physical causes are not covered by this bound. They stay in the existing `physical_geometry` account.
   - *Control:* an island in one place (s = 242) falls to one action.
2. **L is fixed by gate G1.** L = D + 32, where D runs from onset to stable physical return, including detection.
3. **Rung-4 consistency: the island premise is itself weaker before twilight.** Under the same physical premises the
   k = 3 certificate admits a strictly larger coalition budget.
   - With B = 0 and D = 8, the admissible rate is 7.2 against TW-1's 2.958 site-costs per date at F_other = 0, and
     5.4 against 1.972 at F_other = 1.
   - With any one k = 3 change reverted (k, tolerance or repair time), the other two still beat TW-1.
   - Reverting all three removes the gain.

**Effect on the theorem.** P-K3 is no longer primitive. It follows from five things:
- e_site, the cost floor for disabling one site;
- s, the siting rule (D-SPREAD);
- (B, r), the coalition's rolling resource envelope;
- D, the time from onset to return;
- F_other, the cap on losses from other causes.

**Still open.** Measuring e_site, D and (B, r) in our world. Codex's audit stands: the envelope (B, r) cannot be
inferred from a share of power alone, and stockpiled or prepared resources must be inside B.
