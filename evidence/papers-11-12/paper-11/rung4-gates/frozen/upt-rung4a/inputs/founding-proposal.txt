#### 3. Mathematical Formulation of the 21 Failure Accounts

The aggregate liability across all 21 failure accounts is strictly bounded by the system's capital reserve $K(t)$ and protection margin $\Delta_{\text{prot}}(t)$:

$$\sum_{i=1}^{21} \mathcal{FA}_i(t) \le K(t) \cdot \left(1 - \frac{1}{\Delta_{\text{prot}}(t)}\right)$$

##### Key Breakdown of Uncovered Accounts:

1. **Pre-Activation Protection ($\mathcal{FA}_1$):**

$$\mathcal{FA}_1 = N_{\text{pending}} \times \left( C_{\text{crypto}} + 1.45 \times \text{MN2} \right)$$



*Ensures nodes are fully covered against adversarial attacks before they begin processing state transactions.*
2. **Provider Withdrawal Accounting ($\mathcal{FA}_4$):**

$$\mathcal{FA}_4 = \sum_{j \in \text{withdrawn}} \text{Bond}_j \times \left(1 - e^{-\frac{t_{\text{notice}}}{T_{\text{rec}}}}\right)$$



*Penalizes abrupt node exits to fund immediate re-routing and state cloning.*
3. **Repair SLA Overrun ($\mathcal{FA}_{17}$):**

$$\mathcal{FA}_{17} = \max\left(0, t_{\text{downtime}} - t_{\text{SLA}}\right) \times \gamma_{\text{penalty}}$$



*Calculates exact financial debits when hardware repairs exceed the maximum recovery window $T_{\text{rec}}$.*
4. **Twilight Emergency Pre-Transition ($\mathcal{FA}_{21}$):**

$$\mathcal{FA}_{21} = \Phi_{\text{crit}} \cdot \lambda_{\text{decay}} \cdot \mathcal{A}_{\text{min}}$$



*Locked reserve ensuring the system can fund emergency mode shifts at $T_{12}$ without external capital injection.*
