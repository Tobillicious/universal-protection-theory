# JW2.4 — the additional contract behind the single-island point

19 October 2026, Pacific/Auckland. These are requirements for a conditional construction, not measurements or a supplied physical implementation. The old JW2.3 counterexample still applies without these requirements. All inherited valuation, provider-withdrawal, independent-fault, cryptographic and common-failure premises remain necessary.

1. **State placement.** Each protected state version has one primary image and one complete encrypted mirror on a different island. This is the systematic repetition erasure code [2, 1, 2]: rate 1/2, one-erasure tolerance. A complete encrypted image is bounded by one normalized state unit, here interpreted as at most 256MiB. The mirror mapping is a permutation of island primary populations, so no island mirrors more than 1035 states at the k=1 point. Source and mirror are never the same island. Coding concerns ciphertext; it does not authorize a second execution.

2. **Local durability.** Each outer ciphertext image and each island's key share is held on 71 distinct physical compute hosts of that island. The cyclic placement bounds any physical host by 355 mirror records at the point. Under at most 70 whole-window compute losses, at least one of every 71-holder set remains. All surviving targets receive the authenticated generation; delivery never depends on trying failed targets serially. Two durable/staging generations are paid. A generation pinned by recovery is not discarded before recovery acknowledges a successor. Whole-window availability must cover capture, export, recovery, completion overlap and reseeding; instantaneous uptime is insufficient.

3. **Key-only sharing.** Generate a fresh 512-bit state-encryption key and independent fresh Shamir coefficients with each checkpoint. Split the key into 32 independent 16-bit limbs, and share each limb with degree 1 polynomials over GF(2^16), polynomial 0x1100b. Use three distinct island shareholders including the primary and two other islands. Every outer share is 512 bits; two reconstruct. Losing the primary leaves two foreign shares. Local 71-host replication protects each island share. The code checks irreducibility and the interpolation/bijection facts; deterministic checker coefficients are examples, not production randomness.

4. **Secrecy and refresh.** Throughout the entire lifetime of every retained key generation, including staging, recovery and old-generation overlap, an adversary learns at most one distinct island share for that generation. This is a separate share-exposure condition; it does not follow from a cap on disabled islands. Fresh independent keys and coefficients prevent combining different generations' shares into one secret. Retirement securely erases obsolete keys/shares, and rollback cannot recover authority or retired secrets. Entropy generation, full ciphertext confidentiality/authentication, all metadata and offline work must satisfy the uniform conditional 459-effective-bit C-SEQ functionality .512 material bits alone do not prove that functionality. The complete fresh 1024-bit key/coefficient package costs at most 1e-4 compute; GF(2^16) multiply or add costs at most 127/10^9 compute. These are explicit performance targets.

5. **Release.** Two shares are delivered only through sealed channels to the uniquely authorized attested execution instance. Whole-key reconstruction occurs only inside that instance. An ordinary host/operator never receives a whole key. The instance authenticates state version, generation, identity, epoch, nonce, recipient and the source/placement commitment. Reconstruction does not itself authorize execution. The primary's full key also stays inside attested execution under capture; threshold sharing does not protect a primary whose trusted execution is physically broken.

6. **Global execution authority.** Extend the local 172-of 242 maximum-promised-expiry journal with a 201-of 400 island journal. Local grants require current pool authority; a local quorum alone cannot renew indefinitely across a partition. Any two pool quorums intersect in at least two islands; with at most one bad island at least one intersecting signer obeys durable locks. Pending and partially assembled grants are included in maximum promised expiry. The successor waits 49/256 dates where required, uses half-open execution, and permanently retires old authority. Trusted clock error remains<=1/1024 across pause, power loss, reboot, capture and rollback. Unique assignment, progress and durable commits are requirements of an actual protocol, not consequences of the intersection count.

7. **Composed journal latency.** Within the existing 1/8-date verification/journal phase, the extended service must complete the required local and pool work jointly under duty priority, including ingress policing and durable updates. Two separate 1/8 promises do not establish this joint 1/8 bound. The inventory conservatively adds 32 certified pool votes and 32 certified pool roots per island/date; two export and two mirror-durability roots are also paid. Ordinary authenticated submessages are separately counted. The checker prices capacity for this contract; it does not supply a consensus implementation or prove round latency. All new software, clocks and journal realization must fit the same joint 10^-7 common/premise allowance.

8. **Checkpoint freshness.** Capture every 3/4 date with export/durable availability within 1/4 date, giving recoverable state age at most 1 at the relevant cut. The trusted calendar must bound local receive bursts by 71 images, distribute forwarding roles over 172 serving hosts, and satisfy the modeled queues. The service calculation includes source copy, remote propagation, 70-target fanout, receive backlog, local propagation<=1/4096 and durable commit. Most importantly, freshness must hold under overlapping allowed primary faults and island withdrawal, not only on an uninterrupted source. Failover's0.95 end-to-end clock starts at the first relevant disruption and is not restarted by an intermediate failure. The period/traffic arithmetic alone does not prove this joint freshness property; it is an additional functional contract. Without it C3 coverage remains unestablished.

**Bulk-read contract.** The recovery interface must maintain a maximum of seven complete-image source reads per serving mirror host at this point, account for partial-transfer retries, and preserve the composed completion bound under faults during reading. A complete adaptive read/partial-retry implementation is not supplied; it is part of the required joint service contract, not hidden in the matching lemma.

## Additional batching contract for task 2

Use three canonical manifests per destination for exile care, relocation/authority and command quotas. Each has at most 344 strictly ordered unique identities; a tuple binds identity, epoch, destination and expiry, plus authenticated parent/placement proofs. The attested validator checks every entry and emits a root bound to the immutable validator program and state. Charge per entry 16 field operations, eight admitted verification units, 32 stored 256-bit fields and 1024 payload bytes. Each committee job verifies the validator's authenticated root at the existing per-job cost; it does not re-inspect every row. Root creation and complete per-entry validation must satisfy those price targets.

This C-BATCH assumption is essential: a list under a hash alone is not a work-saving certificate. If committee jobs must each inspect all entries at the old per-history cost, work remains linear and the unbatched limit persists. The finite canonical parser checks shape, ordering, capacity and duplicates; it is not attestation code or a proof system. Root forgery and validator bugs belong in the existing crypto/common accounts under the expanded contract.
