# What the sharing checks establish

The robust decoder checks the advertised coding primitive: with n at least 3t+1 and at most t corrupt shares, a degree-t polynomial is uniquely recoverable from the supplied correctly indexed shares. The small exhaustive instance uses n=4, t=1 and field 5; it tests 400 single-corruption cases, rejects 100 false openings and exhibits 300 ambiguities at n=3. The general distance argument is analytical: two degree-t polynomials agree at at most t points, so their codewords differ at at least n-t positions. Two radius-t corruption balls cannot meet when n-t>2t. Erasures and delivery deadlines must be included in the particular recovery contract.

A decoder is not by itself a verifiable sharing protocol. `checks/vss_check.py` exhausts the specified degree-one bivariate assignment space over field 5, checking honest-row consistency and one-receiver privacy. `checks/vss_scope_check.py` independently reconstructs symmetric bivariate extensions from pair-consistent rows over fields 5 and 7 and checks that their single-receiver view distributions are uniform for every secret. Two honest pair-consistent rows uniquely determine the polynomial in this primitive. The old unchecked-third-row control demonstrates missing checks, not a failure of that uniqueness fact.

These results do not implement malicious-dealer complaint resolution, common acceptance, authenticated broadcast, private delivery, binding setup, validated reconstruction or deadlines. Exact lottery fairness is conditional on the complete perfect private/binding sharing resource and communication/entropy/order contracts. A statistical implementation supplies its complete conditional error budget, rather than obtaining one from a population inequality alone.

For the distributed fairness obstruction, the cited primary result is Richard Cleve's 1986 finite-round processor model, [paper](https://courses.grainger.illinois.edu/cs598dk/fa2019/Files/cleve1986.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/12130.12168). The preprint now restricts that citation to its model. It does not assert an impossibility for a protocol endowed with an additional trusted fair resource, or a one-option choice.
