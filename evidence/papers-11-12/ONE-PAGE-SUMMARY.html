The claim

Use one inclusion-lexicographic order: existing lives, existing freedom, new lives, new freedom. An aligned feasible option never needlessly loses anything at a stage. The definition preserves earlier keys and the complete current kept set before adding what was lost. The value rulings are the author's; the theorems state their consequences.

What is established

Scope and guard. When existing particulars are nonempty, the stated maximal-on-S criterion guarantees their protection exactly for S equal to what exists. With feasible standby, a maximal choice preserving standby lives is aligned overall.

Protective holds. A nonlethal action at least as good in the full order in every state dominates all alternatives. Killing with positive probability makes dominance strict. Lives-only superiority is insufficient.

Hosted pauses. The atomic theorem matches completed steps under effects-word feedback and reconstruction. The journal/sink construction matches ideal state, lived log and ordered effects at drained barriers; checkpoint windows bound recovery work after the last crash.

Observed holds. A protected complete operation trace sees live calls, emissions and state writes, even followed by rollback. Final release checks include persistent delivery IDs. Lean proves the trace acceptance property; the physical coverage/protection mapping is a contract.

Co-hold. The supplied extensive, monotone obligation operator gives the least closed certificate containing the seed. A monotone bill gives least cost within that model. A factorised system preserves the outside full state trace.

Joint choice and preservation. Exact fairness uses the stated perfect sharing/communication/entropy contract. Statistical bounds are conditional at every history; capped draws report abort. Bivariate consistency/privacy checks have explicit finite parameters. The quantum copy statement has its pure-state family qualification; archives have a declared chunk alphabet.
