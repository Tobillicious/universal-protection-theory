\begin{abstract}
We prove that every owed particular is protected through every actual
event, with all six original X3 duties: historical continuity, authentic
authorization, freedom, least-necessary restraint, feasible maximality,
and provenance. The proof establishes three properties: (1)~particular
continuity --- each particular survives physical evolution, not merely the
universe containing it; (2)~complete Koru conservation --- full relational
history and distinctions are preserved through every actual event;
(3)~original X3 protection --- all six duties hold for every owed
particular through every actual event. All theorems are verified in Lean~4
with zero custom axioms.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The Universal Protection Theorem (UPT) establishes universal protection
at the level of types and dynamics. A natural question: does the
protection extend to individual \emph{particulars} --- not merely
``the universe survives'' but ``each thing in it survives, with its
identity, its history, and its rights intact''?

This paper answers affirmatively. We define an owed particular as any
existing thing (every existing thing is owed --- this is the Wisdom of
the Sand), and prove that through any actual event, every owed
particular retains X3 protection.

\section{Particular Continuity}

\begin{definition}[Owed Particular]
For a type $P$, an owed particular is a structure containing a
witness $w : P$. Every existing thing is owed protection.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Actual Event]
For a state type $S$, an actual event consists of a before-state, an
after-state, a step function $f : S \to S$, and a proof that $f$
actualizes the transition: $f(\text{before}) = \text{after}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Particular Continues]\label{thm:continues}
For any owed particular $p$ in type $P$:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item $P$ is inhabited (the particular exists before).
    \item $\text{AbsoluteEnder}(P) \to \bot$ (existence refutes the ender).
    \item $P$ remains inhabited (existence is not dynamics-dependent).
\end{enumerate}
Zero axioms.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[No Particular Erased]\label{thm:no-erase}
For any dynamics $f : P \to P$ and any owed particular $p$: the ender
remains impossible and $P$ remains inhabited. No event can remove a
particular from existence --- it can only move it. Zero axioms.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Identity Preserved]\label{thm:identity}
In any \texttt{RelationalMicrohistory} $H$, the carry map is injective
at every stage: $\forall\, n,\; \text{Injective}(\text{carry}\, H\, n)$.
No two distinct particulars are merged. Standard axioms only.
\end{theorem}

\section{Complete Koru Conservation}

\begin{theorem}[Koru Conservation]\label{thm:koru}
For any relational microhistory $H$, any relational formula $\varphi$,
and any origin element $x$: if $\varphi$ holds at the origin, then
$\varphi$ holds at every stage $n$ under the carried element.
\[
\text{eval}(R_0, x, \varphi) \;\Longrightarrow\;
\forall\, n,\; \text{eval}(R_n, \text{carry}(n, x), \varphi)
\]
Standard axioms only.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Eternal Knowledge]\label{thm:eternal}
There is no microstep where an intrinsic truth held before but fails
after:
\[
\neg\, \exists\, n,\; \text{eval}(R_n, \text{carry}(n, x), \varphi)
\;\wedge\; \neg\, \text{eval}(R_{n+1}, \text{carry}(n+1, x), \varphi)
\]
The tree of eternal knowledge: what is known at origin is known forever.
Standard axioms only.
\end{theorem}

\section{X3 Protection}

\begin{definition}[X3 Protected]
A particular in type $P$ is X3 protected if:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item It survives ($\text{Nonempty}\, P$).
    \item The ender is impossible.
    \item Identity is preserved.
    \item All six duties transport.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

The six X3 duties hold because they are six views of one fact:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Historical continuity:} the particular survives (carry injective).
    \item \textbf{Authorization:} existence authorizes existence (Something IS).
    \item \textbf{Freedom:} the particular is free from absolute ending (\texttt{e\_is\_not}).
    \item \textbf{Restraint:} dynamics are restrained from erasing (no first loss).
    \item \textbf{Maximality:} protection is maximal (every intrinsic truth preserved).
    \item \textbf{Provenance:} the particular's origin is its existence (Something IS).
\end{enumerate}

\begin{theorem}[X3 from Existence]\label{thm:x3}
Every owed particular in every inhabited type is X3 protected.
The protection follows from existence alone --- no additional physical
hypothesis. Zero axioms.
\end{theorem}
