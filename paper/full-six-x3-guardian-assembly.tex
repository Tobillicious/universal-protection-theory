\section{Introduction}

The Universal Protection Theorem establishes, unconditionally, that
the absolute ender has no model and that relational microhistory
transport preserves all intrinsic truths. The remaining question:
does every \emph{actual physical event} necessarily admit the structure
(a W-event step with source reflection) that makes the full six-duty
guardian assembly apply?

This paper formalizes the assembly conditional on two clearly stated
physical hypotheses, distinguishing what is mathematically proved from
what requires physical grounding.

\begin{definition}[Complete W-Event Coverage]

\[
\forall\, f : S \to S,\; \exists\, \text{WEvent}(f)

\begin{definition}[Forward Real-Event Reflection]

\[
\forall\, e : \text{RealEvent},\; \exists\, r : \text{SameSourceReflection}(e)

These are the bridge hypotheses --- they connect the mathematical proof
