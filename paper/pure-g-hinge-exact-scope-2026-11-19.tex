\section{Exact Scope of the Pure $G$ Hinge}
\label{sec:pureg-exact-scope}

\paragraph{Constructive proof and axiom status.}
We formalise the Pure $G$ process/contraction hinge in natural-number
arithmetic, without additional physical postulates, Hilbert-space
assumptions, or additive $\Delta$ slack in the radial quasi-hinge.
Theorem \texttt{triadic\_g\_bridge}, the radial six-model-invariant
bundle, \texttt{koru\_corresponds\_transitive}, and fixed-point
persistence have no reported axiomatic dependencies.  At the recorded
Lean~4.35.0 CI head, the $N$-step contraction, successive-envelope,
zero-distance correspondence, and zero-pinning theorems report the
standard Lean axiom \texttt{propext} under \texttt{\#print axioms}.
None of these reports contains \texttt{sorryAx}.  We therefore do
\emph{not} describe the entire collection as literally axiom-free.
The exact dependency lists should be regenerated at the publication
commit if tactics or source imports are changed.

\paragraph{Radial Koru correspondence versus Complete Koru.}
For the contraction pillar $\mathcal C$ with distinguished fixed point
$G$, the mathematical relation
\[
 x\mathrel{\mathcal R_G}y
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 d(x,G)\,k_{\mathrm{den}}
 \leq d(y,G)\,k_{\mathrm{den}}
\]
is the \emph{fixed-point radial Koru correspondence} appearing as
\texttt{KoruCorresponds} in the research Lean module. It is a preorder
on radial measurements; it is not asserted to be the complete,
lossless Koru of the matterspacetime whole. In particular,
two distinct states may have equal radial measurements, in which
case both directions of $\mathcal R_G$ hold while the states remain
distinct. Original Complete Koru retains objectively distinct
particulars, operators, the order and enactment of events, full
histories, causal relations and provenance. For the separately
constructed finite W source, see the existing whole-preservation
theorems \texttt{objective\_wholes\_do\_not\_merge},
\texttt{objective\_Koru\_is\_exact\_under\_W}, and
\texttt{event\_view\_commutes\_with\_objective\_W}.
These source laws are not redefined by the radial interface.
The first spacetime Koru, Complete matterspacetime Koru and forward
spiral Time Koru remain conceptually distinct.

\paragraph{Fixed-point uniqueness at its precise strength.}
The pillar supplies a fixed point $G$ and its fixed-point equation
$\phi(G)=G$. The contraction proves that any other supplied fixed
point $G'$ obeys
\[
 \phi(G')=G'\quad\Longrightarrow\quad d(G,G')=0.
\]
Equality as \emph{states} follows under the additional point-separation
property
\[
 \forall x,y,\quad d(x,y)=0\Longrightarrow x=y.
\]
The additive theorem \texttt{koru\_fixed\_point\_eq\_if\_separated}
checks that implication directly, without assuming state equality or
changing the existing contraction pillar. Neither zero-distance
uniqueness nor conditional state uniqueness alone constructs a fixed
point on an arbitrary nonempty type; the pillar already includes
$G$ and $\phi(G)=G$.

\paragraph{Exact $N$-step result retained.}
For each $N\in\mathbb N$ the already proved radial model inequality is
\[
 d\bigl(\pi(f^N(s_0)),G\bigr)\,k_{\mathrm{den}}^N
 \leq k_{\mathrm{num}}^N\,d\bigl(\pi(s_0),G\bigr).
\]
The no-$\Delta$ mathematical model theorem and zero pinning remain
unchanged. The six \emph{model} invariants here are distinct from UPT's
original six authentic X3 duties.

\paragraph{G3 integration: find before build.}
The larger G3 assembly is not re-proved here. The already derived
\texttt{ConstructivePositiveWGuardian} discharges both existing
coverage and formal six-reflection interfaces for a nonempty
mathematical W model. The external-effect assembly
\texttt{G3SameEventCapstone} and
\texttt{G3BoundedGuardianDischarge} preserve the original six duties
but retain independently supplied physical-domain refinement and
authentic subject-meaning premises (\texttt{FourCauseJointRefinement}
and \texttt{FaithfulRead} in the bounded case). Pure $G$ radial
contraction does not silently supply those premises; no original
X3, full Koru, W or Hume--Hillary definition is weakened.
