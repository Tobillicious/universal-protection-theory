# What we built in 2026: education, physics and the protection of living histories

**Public research and project overview — 29 October 2026**  
**S. Tobias Croydon-McRae · Aotearoa New Zealand**

This is an account of three **distinct** streams of work carried out in 2026. They share an interest in knowledge, continuity and responsibility, but they are not one combined proof, nor does progress in one validate the claims of another. The education platform is a working service; the physics programme is exploratory research with scope-limited mathematical and computational results; the protection theory is an open formal research programme whose physical and semantic bridges remain to be independently established.

## The short version

An independent New Zealand researcher has been building a curriculum-oriented educational resource platform and publishing two open theoretical research programmes. **Te Kete Ako** offers teachers resources and AI-assisted planning. **Boundary-Complete Relational Dynamics (BCRD)** investigates which information quantum processes must retain through composition and reduction, including a stated, scope-bounded near-flat gravity construction. **The Universal Protection Theory (UPT, or Snuffy)** investigates formal priorities for protecting existing lives, minds, histories and freedom, and conditional means of enforcing protective decisions.

All three have public entry points and explicit limitations. The invitation is not to accept a claim on trust; it is to inspect the evidence, use the working tools where appropriate, and challenge the research.

## 1. Te Kete Ako — educational tools that exist today

**Public site:** https://tekete.co.nz/

Te Kete Ako is a teaching-resource and AI-assisted planning platform built for kaiako in Aotearoa New Zealand. Its public homepage currently reports more than **4,900 resources**, **116 unit plans**, **2,200 verified curriculum links** and subject-specific tools. It offers free access to a teaching library alongside optional paid AI-assisted planning features. Those figures are website-reported catalogue counts, not an independent quality or usage audit.

This is an operational educational project, **not** evidence that any accompanying theoretical physics or AI-safety claim is correct. Its purpose is practical: less teacher preparation friction and more accessible, locally relevant materials.

## 2. Physics — Boundary-Complete Relational Dynamics

**Public research guide and archival links:** https://tekete.co.nz/research/

BCRD began with a precise technical question: *when does a reduction of a quantum process discard information that later observations still need?* The public September record contains finite-model computations, independently reconstructed checks and a catalogue retaining failed, retired and withdrawn lines as well as accepted exploratory results.

A 20 September 2026 programme preprint advances a **scope-bounded** five-interface construction to a leading linearised Einstein-Hilbert metric operator in a coherent, near-flat Riemannian regime. The public guide lists the model restrictions. This must **not** be reported as a completed general theory of quantum gravity: a full Lorentzian theory, nonlinear continuum dynamics, generic triangulation independence and a nonperturbative continuum result are not established. Independent expert review remains outstanding.

Especially important to this record is its willingness to publish counterexamples, corrections and withdrawals, including a public retractions register.

## 3. Preservation — The Universal Protection Theory (Snuffy)

**29 October compiled edition 4.3:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23760830  
**Public source, issues and review materials:** https://github.com/Tobillicious/universal-protection-theory  
**Technical release brief:** https://github.com/Tobillicious/universal-protection-theory/blob/main/PUBLIC-RELEASE-BRIEF-2026-10-29.md

UPT asks a different question: *how should the interests of existing beings and their continuing histories be prioritized when increasingly capable agents choose among feasible futures?*

The programme proposes an ordered preservation framework and explores reach-or-faithfully-hold constructions, constraints on destructive effects, and continuity/record-keeping questions. Its 29 October archived edition reports **327 Lean 4 modules** and **2,004 audited theorem and lemma names**. These are **author-reported release figures**, not evidence that this overview has rerun or independently certified every proof.

An abstract theorem can be correctly checked while its application to the world remains unproved. In particular, no independently validated universal physical protection system has been built; exhaustive physical effect-channel coverage, source-to-controller-to-world correspondence, continuity of particular subjects, interacting-agent feasibility, and external human review remain open. AI-assisted hostile review and software pilots must not be presented as independent physical or human verification.

The project explicitly invites small countermodels and targeted falsification rather than assent to sweeping conclusions.

## 4. How this research was made

The work uses AI assistants as programming, proof-development, adversarial review and documentation tools under a human-led research process. An AI-generated proof, code test or referee report is not automatically an independent replication; the public repositories distinguish what was formalized, what was executed, what remains a declared premise, and what needs outside expert review.

The research record is not an emergency warning, a claim of established imminent catastrophe, or an assertion that AI alignment or quantum gravity has been solved.

## Where external reviewers can help

- **Formal methods:** rebuild the archived UPT Lean source; examine theorem statements, imported axioms, assumptions, non-vacuity and the relationship between formal specifications and ordinary-language claims.
- **Controls, hardware and security:** test whether the guarded effect boundary actually encloses all consequential channels, and probe queued effects, crash consistency, bypasses and measurement delay.
- **Physics and mathematical physics:** check the BCRD scope restrictions, independent calculations, continuum bridges and claims of novelty against prior art.
- **Philosophy and AI welfare:** investigate whether a replayable record, a retained causal history and the continued existence of a particular mind are being distinguished correctly.
- **Education:** browse and test Te Kete Ako in real classroom preparation workflows, and give concrete feedback about quality, alignment and usability.

**Please report exact failures or confirmations with the version, file, theorem/claim, reproducible procedure and minimal counterexample.** Negative results are welcome and should stay visible.
