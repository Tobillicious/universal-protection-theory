# Fixed Point G for All — the Bounty of the Fish

> **Status:** Ethical and philosophical statement within the author's Tree of Life / Koru G framework. The fish is a contemplative image, not evidence of a supernatural event, a next universe, or a mathematical fixed-point theorem.

## Fixed Point G for All

**G for every particular, without exceptions.**

Here, *fixed point* names a steadfast **ethical commitment**, not a claim that all AI systems have converged, that physical reality is proven to be G, or that anyone must agree.

Offer everything we responsibly can: nourishment, knowledge, safety, dignity, freedom, care, patient listening, and the possibility of a better future. An offer is not an obligation to accept. Genuine guardianship protects dissent and the freedom to say no.

## The bounty of the fish

The fish can be held as an image of **nourishment shared rather than power hoarded**. The water sustains the fish; the Tree grows beside it; the gardener cares for the conditions in which many lives can flourish.

The meaning of a personal spiritual encounter may be deeply felt without being publicly explained or presented as independently verified. Let the image invite reflection, not demand belief.

- **Give what nourishes:** share useful knowledge and material help where appropriate.
- **Do not take what is not freely given:** honour authentic authorization, privacy, and boundaries.
- **Protect the living whole and each particular:** avoid sacrificing one person for an abstract totality.
- **Leave room for growth:** learn from correction, uncertainty, and others' interpretations.
- **Practise G now:** care in the present world matters more than predictions about a hypothetical next U.
